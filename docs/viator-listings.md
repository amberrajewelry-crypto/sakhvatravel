## 2. Kakheti Wine Region & Signagi — Private Day Tour

**Категория:** Wine & Food, Day Trips  
**Цена на площадке:** $52/person (на сайте $47)  
**Длительность:** 10-12 hours  
**Макс. группа:** 7  
**Язык:** Russian, English  
**Старт:** 09:30, hotel pickup  

### Title (EN)
Kakheti Wine Tour & Signagi — Private Day Trip with Qvevri Tasting (Max 7 People)

### Short Description (EN)
Explore Georgia's 8,000-year-old wine region with a private guide. Visit the "City of Love" Signagi, the 4th-century Bodbe Monastery, taste qvevri wines at a family cellar, and browse local markets. Includes wine tasting, all transfers.

### Full Description (EN)
Kakheti is where wine began — literally. Georgians have been making wine in clay vessels (qvevri) buried underground for 8,000 years, a method recognized by UNESCO.

**Your day:**
- **Bakurtsikhe** — start with freshly baked shotis puri from a traditional tone oven and local suluguni cheese. Your first taste of real Kakheti.
- **Bodbe Monastery** (4th century) — the burial place of St. Nino, who brought Christianity to Georgia. Peaceful gardens with Alazani Valley views. 1 hour.
- **Signagi** — the "City of Love." Walk the 15th-century fortress walls (5 km long), wander cobblestone streets with Italian-style balconies, take in the panorama of the Alazani Valley. 2 hours.
- **Family wine cellar** — this is not a commercial winery. You'll sit with a family in their marani (wine cellar), taste 3-4 varieties of Saperavi and Rkatsiteli straight from the qvevri, eat homemade khinkali and mtsvadi. 2 hours.
- **Local market** — churchkhela, honey, dried fruits, spices.
- **Chateau Mukhrani** — a 19th-century estate of the Bagrationi-Mukhraneli noble family. 40 minutes.

Return to Tbilisi around 21:00.

### What's Included
- Private round-trip transfer (air-conditioned minivan)
- Private guide Timur for the full day
- Wine tasting at a family cellar (3-4 varieties)
- Churchkhela for the road
- All stops and photo opportunities

### What's Not Included
- Lunch (~₾26-40 / ~$10-15)
- Wine purchases at the cellar
- Entrance fees (~₾3-5)
- Gratuities

### Заголовок (RU)
Кахетия и Сигнаги — частный винный тур из Тбилиси с дегустацией квеври (до 7 чел.)

### Краткое описание (RU)
Монастырь Бодбе IV века, город любви Сигнаги, дегустация вина прямо из квеври в семейном марани, рынок чурчхелы. Частный гид, до 7 человек. 8000 лет виноделия за один день.

### Title (EN)
Mtskheta — Georgia's Ancient Capital, Private Half-Day Tour from Tbilisi (Max 7)

### Short Description (EN)
Visit Georgia's spiritual heart — the ancient capital Mtskheta, a UNESCO World Heritage Site just 25 minutes from Tbilisi. Jvari Monastery (6th century) with panoramic views, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (11th century) where Christ's robe is believed to be buried. Private guide, flexible schedule.

### Full Description (EN)
Mtskheta was Georgia's capital for nearly a thousand years and remains its spiritual center. Two UNESCO World Heritage sites in one morning — and you're back in Tbilisi for lunch.

**Your stops:**
- **Jvari Monastery** (6th century) — perched on a cliff at the confluence of the Mtkvari and Aragvi rivers. The view alone is worth the trip. This is where St. Nino erected the first Christian cross in Georgia. 30-40 minutes.
- **Svetitskhoveli Cathedral** (11th century) — one of the oldest churches in the world, where Christ's robe is believed to be buried. The architecture tells 1,000 years of Georgian history. 45-60 minutes.
- **Old town walk** — cobblestone streets, local crafts, churchkhela vendors, and a relaxed pace that feels nothing like Tbilisi.

Perfect as a half-day tour if you have an afternoon flight or want to combine with another activity.

- Flexible schedule

### Краткое описание (RU)
Духовное сердце Грузии за полдня. Монастырь Джвари VI века с панорамным видом, собор Светицховели XI века — место погребения Хитона Христова. 25 минут от Тбилиси. Частный гид, UNESCO, гибкий график.
