Georgia is a country you cannot truly understand in a single day. Tbilisi, Kazbegi and Kakheti sit in completely different corners of the country, and each one deserves its own unhurried time. Day trips are a compromise: you see a lot but feel very little. A multi-day tour in Georgia is an entirely different dimension of travel.

When you stay overnight in Kazbegi, the morning belongs to you — no tourist coaches at Gergeti Trinity Church, no crowds, just the glacier, the silence and the cold clear air at 2200 metres. When you sleep in Kakheti, that means evening wine directly from a family qvevri after the winery closes to tourists, bread baked in a tone oven by the host that morning. The difference between a day trip and a multi-day tour is the difference between a postcard and being there.

Our Georgia packages are designed by guide Timur after three years of living in the country. The 3, 5 and 7-day itineraries are not standard bus programmes — they are private journeys in small groups (up to 7 people) where the pace is yours, not the schedule's. Airport pickup, all transfers, all excursions and accommodation assistance are handled for you.

Sample 7-Day Itinerary — Day by Day

The full week plan for Georgia. Shorter formats use the first N days plus an adapted ending.

Day 1
Arrival • Tbilisi — First Impressions
Airport pickup. Hotel check-in. Evening walk through the Old Town: Abanotubani sulphur baths, Narikala fortress lit up at night, the Bridge of Peace over the Kura. Dinner at one of Tbilisi's best khinkali restaurants.
Old Tbilisi tour Night Tbilisi
Day 2
Tbilisi — Into the Capital
Full day in Tbilisi: Avlabari and the Armenian quarter, funicular up to Mtatsminda, Rustaveli Avenue, Deserter's Bazaar. Evening: traditional Georgian family dinner — khachapuri, khinkali, homemade wine, real hospitality away from tourist restaurants.
City overview Family dinner tour
Day 3
Mtskheta • Kazbegi — The Mountains Call
Morning: Mtskheta — Jvari monastery overlooking the confluence of the Aragvi and Kura rivers, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (UNESCO World Heritage). Then north on the Georgian Military Highway: Zhinvali reservoir, Gudauri overlook, Kazbegi town at dusk. Overnight below Kazbek (5047 m).
Mtskheta Kazbegi
Day 4
Kazbegi — Mountain Sunrise
Early rise and hike to Gergeti Trinity Church before the day-trip crowds arrive. The glacier and the church in morning light — this is the view that brings people to Georgia. Afternoon: villages of Gergeti and Stepantsminda, Kazbegi-style khinkali, return drive via Ananuri fortress.
Full Kazbegi day
Day 5
Kakheti — Wine, Monasteries, Sighnaghi
Full day in Georgia's wine heartland. Bodbe monastery (relics of St. Nino), Sighnaghi — a walled hilltop town with views across the Alazani Valley. Wine tasting at a family marani: Rkatsiteli and Saperavi poured straight from qvevri clay jars. Return to Tbilisi in the evening.
Kakheti wine tour
Day 6
Kutaisi — Western Georgia
Transfer to Kutaisi (3 hours). Prometheus Cave — Georgia's largest cave system with underground lakes and coloured stalactites. Gelati monastery (UNESCO, 12th century) — a masterpiece of Georgian medieval architecture. Optional: Martvili Canyon by boat on emerald-green water.
Kutaisi day tour
Day 7
Batumi • Departure
Batumi on the Black Sea: the botanical garden (one of the richest in the world), the Old Town with European-style architecture, the famous boulevard. Flight from Batumi or Kutaisi airport, or drive back to Tbilisi for an evening departure. Optional farewell dinner at a Georgian restaurant.
Batumi tour

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a multi-day tour in Georgia cost?
The 3-day Slow Travel tour starts at ₾595 per person. The 5-day tour runs from approximately ₾900/person, and the 7-day tour from ₾1400/person. Exact pricing depends on group size, season and accommodation preferences. Contact us — we will quote within 15 minutes.
What is included in the tour price?
Included in all packages: airport transfer, all internal transfers by private minivan, private guide service for all days, entrance tickets to all sites on the itinerary, and accommodation assistance. 5 and 7-day packages additionally include a Georgian family dinner and Kakheti wine tasting. Restaurant meals are at your own expense.
Can I customise the itinerary?
Absolutely. All itineraries are fully flexible. Want to swap Batumi for Svaneti, add Borjomi, or stay an extra night in Kazbegi? No problem. The route is planned individually based on your interests and travel dates.
Is a deposit required?
Full prepayment (100%) before the tour starts — your dates are confirmed once paid. Cancellation: 14+ days before — full refund minus non-refundable costs (hotels, tickets); 7–13 days — 30% is withheld; 3–6 days — 50%; less than 3 days — non-refundable. We accept bank transfer, USDT/USDC, or cash in Tbilisi.
When is the best time to visit Georgia?
Best seasons: April–June and September–October. Kazbegi mountains are accessible May–October. Kakheti in September is magical during grape harvest. In winter we run Tbilisi + Kakheti + Kutaisi routes — mountain roads are closed by snow.
Can I travel solo?
Yes, solo travellers are very welcome. Per-person cost is slightly higher (no shared transport costs) but you get a fully private tour of Georgia — the guide adapts every element around you personally.
What accommodation level is included?
We select comfortable 3-star guesthouses and hotels with strong reviews in each region by default. If you prefer 4-5 star or need a budget option, just tell us when planning — we will adjust accordingly.
Why choose a multi-day tour over separate day trips?
Day trips give you a surface view — arrive, see highlights, leave. Multi-day travel means staying overnight in Kazbegi to see the glacier at sunrise, wine from the qvevri after the tourists leave, a morning in Kakheti without tour buses. The difference between a postcard and actually being somewhere.
