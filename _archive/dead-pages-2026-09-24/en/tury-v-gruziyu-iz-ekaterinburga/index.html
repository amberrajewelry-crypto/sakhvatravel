FAQ: Georgia Tours from Yekaterinburg

How do I get from Yekaterinburg to Tbilisi?
No direct flights. Best route: Yekaterinburg → Istanbul (Turkish Airlines) → Tbilisi, ~7–8 hours total, round-trip from ~$200. Alternative: via Dubai (flydubai, ~$240, ~9–11 hours) or via Yerevan + minibus (~$210). Koltsovo Airport has good connections to Istanbul Sabiha.
Do Russian citizens need a visa for Georgia?
No. Russian citizens can stay in Georgia visa-free for up to 365 days. A Russian passport is sufficient. Border crossing usually takes 5–15 minutes.
How much does a 7-day Georgia trip cost from Yekaterinburg?
~$450–725/person for 7 days: flights ~$200, accommodation ~$70, food ~$35, private tours from ~$130. Package tours cost $800–1,200 per person with group-size excursions and less flexibility.
Do Russian cards work in Georgia?
Mir cards do NOT work in Georgia. Bring USD or EUR to exchange in Tbilisi — rates are excellent. Best exchange spots: Rustaveli Avenue and Deserter's Bazaar. Visa/Mastercard from foreign banks works everywhere. TBC Bank and Bank of Georgia ATMs are reliable.
What language are the tours conducted in?
Guide Timur conducts all tours in English. He is fluent, has lived in Tbilisi since 2023, and tailors every tour to international visitors. Groups are up to 7 people — never a crowd.
Can I cancel if my plans change?
Yes. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the tour. Rescheduling is always possible — message guide Timur via WhatsApp or Telegram.
