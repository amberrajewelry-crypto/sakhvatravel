Sakhva Partner Program

How to earn from tours across Georgia

The Sakhva Travel partner program is a simple way to earn from excursions in Tbilisi and across Georgia without running tours yourself. Hotels and guesthouses offer authentic routes to guests right at reception, bloggers and community admins recommend tours to their audience, and guides or drivers pass us guests on dates they can't cover. For every completed booking a partner earns a fixed 10% commission — no upfront cost, no deposits, no website of your own.

You work with a licensed local guide and a catalogue of 67 authentic tours: wine Kakheti, Kazbegi, Borjomi, food programs and masterclasses. Payment is on the tour day, and commission is credited in your dashboard after the tour is confirmed. Send a request — access is approved manually within a day.

Questions

The essentials, briefly

How much do I earn?

A fixed 10% of every completed booking. The rate is the same for all partners — no tiers or volume conditions.

When is the commission accrued?

After the tour takes place and Sakhva confirms it. The accrual is shown in your dashboard commission log.

Do I need investment, a website or a contract?

No. Joining is free and you do not need your own website. Just submit an application — access is approved manually.

How does the guest pay for the tour?

On the tour day, on site — cash, card or crypto. You never handle payments or hold deposits.

Which tours are available?

67 authentic routes across Georgia: day trips and multi-day tours, wine and food programs, master classes.

How do I see my bookings?

In your dashboard: booking statuses and accrued commission. You get a notification for each new booking. A REST API is available for integrations.
