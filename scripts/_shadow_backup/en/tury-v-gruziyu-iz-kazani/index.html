Kazan is a city that blends Islamic and Orthodox traditions — much like Tbilisi, which has coexisted Christian, Muslim and Jewish communities for centuries. Kazan residents often feel a cultural familiarity in Georgia that surprises them. Add no visa, affordable flights via Moscow, and some of the world's best food and wine — and you have one of the most compelling international trips from Tatarstan. Here is a full planning guide for your Georgia trip from Kazan in 2026.

Flights from Kazan to Tbilisi 2026

From Kazan Mayakovsky Airport (KZN) — connections via Moscow or Istanbul.

Flight options: Kazan → Tbilisi

✈️

Via Moscow — main route. Kazan (KZN) → Moscow Sheremetyevo or Domodedovo (2 hrs) + layover 2–4 hrs + Moscow → Tbilisi (2.5–3 hrs). Total 6–9 hours. Airlines: Aeroflot, S7, Pobeda, Georgian Airways. Prices from 20,000 RUB round-trip. Search on Aviasales early for the cheapest Moscow connection.

🇹🇷

Via Istanbul (Turkish Airlines). Kazan → Istanbul (4–5 hrs) + layover 3+ hrs + Istanbul → Tbilisi (1.5 hrs). Total 9–12 hours. From 22,000 RUB. Good option for those who want a comfortable stopover at Istanbul Airport.

💡

Kazan tip: Kazan Airport has good Aeroflot frequency to both Sheremetyevo and Domodedovo. Watch for S7 sales from Kazan — they often offer budget fares on the Kazan–Moscow leg. Book the Moscow–Tbilisi leg separately for maximum flexibility.

🕐

Time difference: Kazan is UTC+3, Tbilisi is UTC+4. Georgia is just 1 hour ahead — the easiest possible adjustment. Morning departure from Kazan means you arrive in Tbilisi by early afternoon, ready to start exploring.

Tips for Kazan Travellers

Practical advice to make the most of your Georgia trip.

💳

Cards and Cash

UnionPay (Gazprombank, RNCB) accepted at most Tbilisi ATMs and shops. Cash RUB and USD exchange well. Russian Visa/Mastercard do not work. Get a UnionPay card before departure.

⏰

Time Zone: +1 Hour

Tbilisi is just 1 hour ahead of Kazan. Practically no adjustment needed. You land almost on the same time you left — no disorientation.

🕌

Multicultural Tbilisi

Kazan visitors often feel at ease in Tbilisi — the city has historic mosques, synagogues and churches side by side. A unique urban harmony that resonates with Kazan's own multicultural identity.

🍷

Food and Wine

Khinkali, khachapuri, lobiani, pkhali. Average lunch with wine: 25–40 GEL. Kakheti winery wine from 8 GEL. Pack 2 bottles in checked luggage — they make great Kazan gifts.

📱

SIM Card

SIM at Tbilisi Airport: 5–8 GEL for 10 days / 5GB data. Providers: Magti, Geocell, Beeline Georgia. Wi-Fi is everywhere in Tbilisi cafes, hotels and parks.

📅

Best Season from Kazan

April–May or September–October for perfect weather and value. Summer is hot (+35°C) — manageable if you plan mountain trips for cooler air. Winter (Dec–Feb): Tbilisi at +8°C is a warm break from Kazan's frost.

FAQ — Georgia Tours from Kazan

How to fly from Kazan to Tbilisi?
Via Moscow: KZN → Moscow (2 hrs) + layover + Moscow → Tbilisi (2.5–3 hrs). Total 6–9 hours, from 20,000 RUB round-trip. Also via Istanbul on Turkish Airlines. No direct KZN–TBS flight currently.
Do I need a visa for Georgia?
No visa required for Russian citizens. Enter with foreign passport or RF internal passport. Stay up to 365 days without a visa.
What is the time difference between Kazan and Tbilisi?
Tbilisi is UTC+4, Kazan is UTC+3. Georgia is just 1 hour ahead. Almost no adjustment needed — easiest possible time zone shift.
What card should I use in Georgia?
UnionPay — get one at Gazprombank or RNCB. Works at most ATMs and shops. Cash RUB also exchanges well. Russian Visa and Mastercard are blocked.
Is Georgia safe for tourists from Kazan?
Yes. Georgia is one of the safest countries in the region. Tbilisi has a very low crime rate. The city is welcoming to visitors from Russia — there is no hostility. The guide will show you the best spots while keeping the experience hassle-free.
