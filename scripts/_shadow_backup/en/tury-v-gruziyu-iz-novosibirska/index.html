Flights from Novosibirsk to Tbilisi 2026

From Tolmachevo Airport (OVB) to Tbilisi — two main routes with one layover.

Flight options: Novosibirsk → Tbilisi

✈️

Via Moscow — the main option. Tolmachevo (OVB) → Sheremetyevo or Domodedovo (3.5–4 hrs) + layover 2–4 hrs + Moscow → Tbilisi (2.5–3 hrs). Total 8–11 hours. Airlines: Pobeda, Aeroflot, S7, Georgian Airways. Price from 22,000 RUB round-trip. Search on Aviasales — Novosibirsk allocation is often cheaper than Moscow origin.

🇹🇷

Via Istanbul (Turkish Airlines / Pegasus). Tolmachevo → Istanbul (5–6 hrs) + layover from 3 hrs + Istanbul → Tbilisi (1.5 hrs). Total 10–13 hours. Price from 24,000 RUB. Istanbul Airport transit zone is large and comfortable.

💡

Tip for Novosibirsk travellers: watch for Pobeda flash sales from Novosibirsk — the base fare Tolmachevo → Moscow sometimes drops to 1,500–2,500 RUB. Your whole Tbilisi trip can then cost 15,000–18,000 RUB round-trip. Telegram channels with deals: @avia_ru_discount.

🕐

Time difference: Novosibirsk is UTC+7, Tbilisi is UTC+4. Georgia is 3 hours behind Siberia. Departing at 08:00 from Novosibirsk, you land in Tbilisi around 13:00 local time — the whole afternoon is free to explore the Old Town.

Tips for Travellers from Novosibirsk

Practical advice from a guide who meets visitors from Siberia every week.

💳

Cards and Cash

UnionPay cards (Gazprombank, RNCB, Rosselkhozbank) work at most ATMs and shops in Tbilisi. Russian Visa and Mastercard do not work. Alternative: cash USD or RUB — Tbilisi exchange offices on Pushkin Street offer excellent rates.

⏰

Time Zone: −3 Hours

Novosibirsk is 3 hours ahead of Tbilisi. Flying west, your body clock runs late — this actually feels comfortable. Morning arrivals let you start sightseeing immediately.

🌡️

Climate and Packing

Tbilisi is warmer than Novosibirsk almost year-round. July–August: +35–38°C, bring light clothing and a hat. For Kazbegi (2,200m altitude): always pack a jacket and walking shoes.

🍷

Food and Wine

Khinkali, khachapuri, pkhali — try everything. Average lunch with wine: 25–40 GEL. Wine in Kakheti from 8–15 GEL per bottle at a winery. You can bring 2 bottles in checked luggage.

📱

SIM Card

SIM card at Tbilisi Airport: from 5–8 GEL for 10 days with 5GB data. Providers: Magti, Geocell, Beeline Georgia. Wi-Fi works everywhere — cafes, hotels, parks.

📅

Best Time to Visit

From Novosibirsk: April–May (spring, green hills, cheaper flights) and September–October (harvest season, +22–27°C, Kakheti wine festival). Winter in Tbilisi is also pleasant — +8–12°C, no crowds.

FAQ — Georgia Tours from Novosibirsk

How to get from Novosibirsk to Tbilisi?
No direct flights. Option 1 — via Moscow: Tolmachevo → Sheremetyevo (3.5 hrs) + layover + Moscow → Tbilisi (2.5 hrs), total 8–11 hrs, from 22,000 RUB round-trip. Option 2 — via Istanbul on Turkish Airlines: Tolmachevo → Istanbul (5–6 hrs) + layover + Tbilisi (1.5 hrs), total 10–13 hrs, from 24,000 RUB.
Do I need a visa to Georgia?
No. Russian citizens do not need a visa. Entry with a foreign passport or Russian internal passport. Maximum stay without a visa is 365 days. At the border state purpose of visit as "tourism".
How long is the flight from Novosibirsk to Tbilisi?
Via Moscow — 8–11 hours total (3.5 hrs + waiting + 2.5 hrs). Via Istanbul — 10–13 hours. Night flights save a day — depart in the evening, arrive in the morning in Tbilisi.
What is the best time to visit Georgia from Novosibirsk?
April–May: blooming Tbilisi, green mountains, cheaper. September–October: harvest, golden Kakheti, +22–27°C. Winter (Dec–Feb): few tourists, lower prices, Tbilisi at +6–12°C — a warm relief from Siberia.
What card to use in Georgia?
UnionPay is the main option. Get one at Gazprombank or RNCB before travelling. Cash RUB or USD also exchange well at Tbilisi offices. UnionPay is accepted at supermarkets, restaurants, hotels and most shops.
What is included in the tour price?
Included: private minivan, English-speaking guide, entrance tickets (fortresses, museums, caves). Food is at your own expense, except for the dinner tour (₾250) and wine tasting in Kakheti. No hidden fees.
How far in advance should I book tours from Novosibirsk?
1–2 weeks ahead is recommended. In peak season (June–September) Kazbegi and Kakheti fill up faster. Booking is free, no deposit, cancellation without penalty up to 24 hours. Message via WhatsApp or Telegram — reply within 15 minutes.
