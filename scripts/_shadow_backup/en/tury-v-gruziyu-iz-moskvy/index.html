Practical Tips for Travellers from Moscow

What to know before you go — visa, money, SIM card, transport.

📄

No Visa Required

Russian citizens can stay in Georgia for up to 365 days without a visa. A Russian passport is sufficient. Border control usually takes 5–15 minutes.

💳

Money & Exchange

Georgia uses the lari (₾). Exchange in Tbilisi — rates are much better than in Moscow. Best exchange offices: Rustaveli Avenue and Deserter's Bazaar. Visa/Mastercard accepted everywhere. Mir cards do NOT work.

📱

SIM Card

Buy at Tbilisi airport. Operators: Magti, Geocell (Silknet), Beeline Georgia. A plan with 5 GB data costs ₾5–10 (~$2–4). Excellent 4G coverage across the whole country.

🚗

Getting Around

In Tbilisi use Bolt taxi (cheaper than local alternatives). To other cities: minibuses from Didube bus station. All tours with guide Timur include comfortable private transport.

FAQ: Georgia Tours from Moscow

How do I get from Moscow to Tbilisi in 2026?
There are no direct flights. The most popular route is via Istanbul with Turkish Airlines (~$160 round-trip, 5–6 hours total). Alternatives: via Dubai (flydubai/Emirates, ~$200), via Yerevan + minibus (~$170), or overland via Vladikavkaz through the Upper Lars border crossing (~$40).
Do Russian citizens need a visa for Georgia?
No. Russian citizens do not need a visa for Georgia and can stay up to 365 days. A Russian passport is sufficient. Border crossing usually takes 5–15 minutes at the checkpoint.
How much does a 7-day Georgia trip from Moscow cost?
An independent trip for 7 days costs ~$410–665/person: flights ~$160, accommodation ~$70, food ~$35, private guide tours from ~$130. This is 25–35% cheaper than package tours which cost $750–1,100 per person.
Do Russian bank cards work in Georgia?
Mir cards do NOT work in Georgia. Bring cash (USD or EUR) to exchange in Tbilisi — rates are far better there than in Moscow. Best exchange spots: Rustaveli Avenue and Deserter's Bazaar. Visa/Mastercard issued by foreign banks work everywhere. TBC Bank and Bank of Georgia ATMs work with foreign cards.
When is the best time to visit Georgia from Moscow?
Best seasons: spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October). May brings blooming nature; September–October is wine harvest season in Kakheti. Summer (June–August) is hot (+35–38°C in Tbilisi). Winter is mild in the city (+5–10°C) and great for skiing at Gudauri.
Which tours are essential for visitors from Moscow?
Top 3: 1) Kazbegi — mountains, Gergeti Trinity Church (from ₾175/person); 2) Kakheti — wine, Sighnaghi, qvevri tasting (from ₾170/person); 3) Mtskheta — UNESCO, Svetitskhoveli (from ₾90/person). For the city: Old Tbilisi walking tour (from ₾90/person).
