Getting from Krasnodar to Tbilisi 2026

The shortest international trip from South Russia — three ways to reach Tbilisi.

Transport options: Krasnodar → Tbilisi

✈️

Direct flight — the fastest option. Krasnodar (KRR) → Tbilisi (TBS), about 2 hours. Georgian Airways operates the route. Prices from 12,000–18,000 RUB round-trip. Book 3–4 weeks ahead for best fares. Krasnodar Airport is compact and boarding is quick.

🚗

By car via Upper Lars. Krasnodar → Vladikavkaz (~4 hrs) → Upper Lars border crossing → Tbilisi (~3 hrs). Total ~7–8 hrs depending on border queue. Queue-free periods: early mornings on weekdays. The Georgian Military Highway (Kazbegi road) is spectacular — consider stopping at Ananuri fortress and Jinvali reservoir on the way.

🚌

Bus/marshrutka. Direct buses from Krasnodar Bus Station to Tbilisi — around 12–14 hours. Comfortable for budget travellers. Tickets from 2,500–4,000 RUB. Several departures per week. Book on Ecolines or local Krasnodar-Tbilisi minibus companies.

💡

Key advantage for Krasnodar residents: Same time zone as Tbilisi (both UTC+3 in summer). No jet lag, no early adjustment — you land and start immediately. Krasnodar cuisine lovers will feel right at home with Georgian food: fresh herbs, grilled meat, adjika.

Tips for Krasnodar Travellers

Practical advice from a guide who welcomes visitors from the Krasnodar Krai regularly.

💳

Cards and Cash

UnionPay (Gazprombank, RNCB) works at most ATMs and shops. Russian Visa/Mastercard do not work. Cash RUB exchanges well — Pushkin Street exchange offices in central Tbilisi offer the best rates.

⏰

Same Time Zone

Krasnodar and Tbilisi are both UTC+3 in summer. Zero jet lag — you land and start immediately. One of the biggest advantages over flying to Turkey or Europe.

🌡️

Climate

Tbilisi has a similar summer to Krasnodar but hotter — July/August hits +35–38°C. Spring and autumn are ideal: +18–25°C. For Kazbegi mountains always bring a jacket (2,200m altitude).

🍷

Food and Wine

Khinkali, khachapuri, lobiani, pkhali. Average restaurant lunch with wine: 25–40 GEL (~800–1,300 RUB). Wine at a Kakheti winery: from 8 GEL a bottle. Bring 2 bottles home in checked luggage.

🚕

Getting Around Tbilisi

Yandex Taxi works in Tbilisi. Metro covers main tourist areas (flat fare 1 GEL). Bolt also available. City is walkable in the Old Town — most sights within 30 min on foot.

📅

Best Time from Krasnodar

April–May and September–October are ideal for weekends. Summer is hot but manageable. Winter getaway: December–February, Tbilisi at +8°C feels mild compared to Russian winter.

FAQ — Georgia Tours from Krasnodar

How far is Krasnodar from Tbilisi?
By air: about 2 hours direct. By car via Upper Lars border: about 7–8 hours (Krasnodar → Vladikavkaz ~4 hrs + border + drive to Tbilisi ~3 hrs). Krasnodar is one of the closest Russian cities to Georgia.
Is there a direct flight from Krasnodar to Tbilisi?
Yes. Georgian Airways operates direct flights KRR → TBS, about 2 hours. Check availability on Georgian Airways website or aggregators. Seasonal frequency varies — book ahead in summer.
Do I need a visa for Georgia from Russia?
No visa needed. Russian citizens enter with a foreign passport or internal RF passport. Free stay up to 365 days.
What card to use in Georgia?
UnionPay — get one at Gazprombank or RNCB before travelling. Cash RUB also exchanges well in Tbilisi exchange offices. Russian Visa and Mastercard are not accepted.
Can I do a weekend trip from Krasnodar to Georgia?
Yes, easily. A long weekend (Friday–Sunday or Saturday–Monday) is enough for 2 tours in Tbilisi — for example, Night Tbilisi + Kazbegi on Saturday + Mtskheta Sunday morning. The 2-hour direct flight makes it very practical.
