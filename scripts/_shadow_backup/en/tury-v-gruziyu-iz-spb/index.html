Georgia from Saint Petersburg is one of the easiest escapes available in 2026. While there are no direct flights, connecting via Istanbul or Dubai gets you to Tbilisi in 6–8 hours — and the contrast couldn't be greater. From the rain and cold of the Neva to warm Caucasian hospitality, street food and ancient monasteries. An independent trip with a private guide gives you far more than any package tour while costing 25–35% less.

FAQ: Georgia Tours from Saint Petersburg

How do I get from Saint Petersburg to Tbilisi?
No direct flights. Best routes: Istanbul via Turkish Airlines or Pegasus (~$170 round-trip, 6–7 hours), Dubai via flydubai (~$210), Yerevan + minibus (~$180). Book 2–3 months ahead for best prices.
Do I need a visa for Georgia from Russia?
No. Russian citizens can stay in Georgia visa-free for up to 365 days. A Russian passport is all you need. Border crossing is quick — 5–15 minutes typically.
What is the weather like in Georgia compared to St. Petersburg?
Georgia is warmer and sunnier than St. Petersburg almost year-round. Tbilisi averages +20–28°C in spring and autumn vs. +8–16°C in SPb. Even in winter, Tbilisi is mild (+5–10°C) while SPb is cold and dark.
How much does a 7-day Georgia trip cost?
~$420–675/person independently: flights ~$170, accommodation ~$70, food ~$35, private tours from ~$130. Package tours cost $800–1,100 with less flexibility and group-size tours.
Can I cancel a tour if my plans change?
Yes. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start. Rescheduling is always possible — just message via WhatsApp or Telegram and guide Timur will sort it out.
