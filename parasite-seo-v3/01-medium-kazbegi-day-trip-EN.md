If you only have time for one trip outside Tbilisi, make it Kazbegi. In a single day you go from a warm Georgian capital to a 2,170-meter mountain village sitting under the glaciers of Mount Kazbek — one of the highest peaks in the Caucasus. I've made this drive more times than I can count, and here is how to do it right in 2026.

## Why Kazbegi is worth the early start

The village (officially Stepantsminda) is famous for one view: the 14th-century **Gergeti Trinity Church** standing alone on a green hill, with the snow cone of Mount Kazbek behind it. It's the kind of place that looks unreal in photos and somehow better in person.

But the road itself is half the trip. The **Georgian Military Highway** climbs through the Aragvi valley, past the Ananuri fortress on a turquoise reservoir, the mineral travertine terraces of Truso, and the Soviet-era "Friendship of Peoples" panorama at the Gudauri pass. You don't just arrive at Kazbegi — you earn it through three hours of mountain scenery.

## The numbers you actually need

The distance from Tbilisi to Kazbegi is **157 km**, and the drive takes **2.5 to 3.5 hours** depending on weather and how often you stop. There are three ways to get there:

- **Marshrutka (shared minibus)** — leaves from Didube bus station, around ₾15 one way. Cheap, but it doesn't stop for photos and you're on its schedule.
- **Full-car taxi** — ₾120–150 for the car, good if you're a group of three or four and want flexibility.
- **Private guided tour** — from around ₾175, with stops at Ananuri, Gudauri, and Gergeti, plus someone who actually explains what you're looking at.

## A realistic day-trip timeline

- **08:00** — leave Tbilisi before traffic.
- **09:30** — first stop at Ananuri fortress and the Zhinvali reservoir.
- **11:00** — Gudauri pass and the panorama viewpoint.
- **12:30** — arrive in Kazbegi, lunch in the village.
- **13:30** — drive or hike up to Gergeti Trinity Church.
- **16:00** — start back, golden light on the mountains.
- **19:00** — back in Tbilisi.

## Things first-timers get wrong

**They underestimate the weather.** It can be 28°C in Tbilisi and 8°C with wind at Gergeti. Bring a layer even in summer.

**They try to drive themselves without snow experience.** In shoulder season the Gudauri section can ice over. If you're not confident on mountain roads, don't risk a rental.

**They skip Gergeti because of the climb.** You don't have to hike — a 4x4 takes you up the last steep section in 15 minutes.

## Final tip

Go on a clear day. Kazbek hides in clouds roughly half the time, and the whole trip changes when the peak is out. Check the mountain forecast (not the Tbilisi one) the night before, and keep your plans flexible. Get that one clear morning, and Kazbegi becomes the photo everyone asks you about when you're home.
