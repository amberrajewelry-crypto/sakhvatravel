About the tour

The Most Iconic Day Trip from Tbilisi

Kazbegi (Stepantsminda) is the must-do day trip from Tbilisi — and for good reason. The route along the Georgian Military Highway passes through some of the most dramatic mountain scenery in the Caucasus: reservoir lakes, alpine villages, ski resorts, and glacier-capped peaks that stretch to 5,047 metres.

The highlight is Gergeti Trinity Church — a 14th-century stone church perched on a ridge at 2,170 metres with an unobstructed view of Mt. Kazbek. You'll have 2.5–3 hours here, enough to hike up and take it all in.

Guide Timur has been running this route for years and knows exactly which stops to make, where to get the best photos, and how to pace the day so it never feels rushed. Maximum group size is 6 people — this is a private tour, not a shared bus.

Day schedule

Route & Timeline

08:00
Hotel pickup in Tbilisi
Timur meets you at your hotel or a central point in Tbilisi. Comfortable AC minivan with all amenities.
10:00
Ananuri Fortress
16th–17th century feudal castle complex overlooking Zhinvali Reservoir. Towers, churches, and sweeping views. 30–40 min stop.
11:30
Gudauri Viewpoint
Georgia's top ski resort at 2,196 m. The lookout point offers a dramatic view of the Greater Caucasus range. Photo stop.
13:00
Stepantsminda village
The town below Gergeti Trinity Church. Lunch break here — try local khinkali (Georgian dumplings). Guide recommends the best spots.
14:00
Gergeti Trinity Church
2.5–3 hours at the church and summit ridge. Hike up (1.5h) or take a local 4WD taxi. Staggering views of Mt. Kazbek. This is the heart of the tour.
~21:00
Return to Tbilisi
Drop-off at your hotel. Total driving time ~5 hours, but the scenery makes it part of the experience.
