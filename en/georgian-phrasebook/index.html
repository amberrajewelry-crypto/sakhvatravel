Georgian alphabet with pronunciation

33 letters, no capitals. Tap a letter to hear its name. More in our Georgian alphabet guide.

How to read the transliteration

  • kh (ხ) — like the ch in Scottish loch.
  • gh (ღ) — a throaty g, close to the French r.
  • q (ყ) — a k pronounced deep in the throat.
  • k, t, p, ts, ch each have two letters: aspirated (ქ თ ფ ც ჩ) and sharp ejective (კ ტ პ წ ჭ). Say them softly and you will still be understood.
  • Stress is light, usually on the first syllable. Every letter is pronounced.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say “thank you” in Georgian?

“Madloba” (მადლობა). The polite form is “gmadlobt” (გმადლობთ) and “thank you very much” is “didi madloba” (დიდი მადლობა). You can hear all three in the Politeness section.

How do you say hello in Georgian?

“Gamarjoba” (გამარჯობა) works for both hello and good day — it comes from the word for victory. The reply is “gagimarjos” (გაგიმარჯოს). In the morning you can also say “dila mshvidobisa”.

Do people in Georgia speak English?

In Tbilisi, Batumi and tourist areas most young people speak some English; older people more often speak Russian. In villages and the mountains a few Georgian phrases help a lot — and locals always appreciate the effort.

Is Georgian hard to read?

Not really: the alphabet has 33 letters, no capitals, and every letter is always pronounced the same way. With the alphabet table on this page you can learn to read signs and menus in an evening.

Can I use the phrasebook offline?

Save the phrase list with transliteration as a PDF with the “Download cheat sheet” button. Audio and the translator need a connection — mobile data in Georgia is cheap and works almost everywhere.

What is the emergency number in Georgia?

112 for ambulance, police and fire. “Help!”, “Call a doctor” and “Call the police” are in the Emergencies section.
