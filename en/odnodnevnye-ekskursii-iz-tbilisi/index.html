Tips for Day Trips from Tbilisi

Six practical things the guide recommends to every guest before their day trip.

1

Depart Early

Most day trips leave at 08:30–09:00. An early start means arriving at key sites before the crowds — especially important at Gergeti Church and Martvili Canyon.

2

Dress in Layers

Georgia's weather changes quickly. Tbilisi can be warm while Kazbegi is cold. Even in summer, bring a jacket for mountain routes.

3

Carry Some GEL Cash

Many small restaurants and market stalls outside Tbilisi only accept Georgian lari. Card readers are rare in village locations.

4

Charge Your Phone

Mobile signal can be weak in mountain areas. Download offline maps before departure. The guide will have you covered, but a charged phone never hurts.

5

Book 1–2 Days Ahead

To guarantee your preferred date — especially in peak season (May–October) and on weekends — booking 2–3 days in advance is recommended.

6

Check Physical Requirements

Some routes involve stairs or uneven terrain (Gergeti, Martvili Canyon steps). Mention any mobility concerns when booking — the guide will adapt.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which day trip from Tbilisi should I choose?
If you have one day: Kazbegi for mountains and drama, Kakheti for wine and culture, Mtskheta for a quick half-day UNESCO trip. If you love nature: Kutaisi (caves and canyons) or Borjomi (national park). For a bucket-list day: Batumi and the Black Sea.
What is included in the price?
All day trips include: private English-speaking guide, comfortable minivan transfer with hotel pickup, entrance tickets to all sites. Food is not included (except wine tasting in Kakheti). You pay for your own meals at restaurants along the route.
How much do day trips from Tbilisi cost?
Prices per person: Mtskheta ₾98 (3–4h), Kakheti ₾170 (10–12h), Kazbegi ₾175 (10–12h), Borjomi ₾178 (10–11h), Kutaisi ₾183 (12–13h), Batumi ₾201 (15h). All prices include guide, transport and entrance tickets.
How far in advance should I book?
1–2 days ahead for weekdays, 3–4 days for weekends in peak season. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure.
What time do day trips depart?
Departure time is agreed with the guide when booking. Most trips leave between 08:00 and 09:30 from your hotel or a convenient central location in Tbilisi.
Are day trips available year-round?
Most routes run year-round. Kazbegi in winter: the road to Gergeti Church can close after heavy snow — the guide checks conditions before every trip. Winter Kazbegi with snow on the mountains is spectacular when roads are clear.
Can I combine two destinations in one day?
Some combinations work well: Mtskheta + Ananuri (half day), Kakheti includes Sighnaghi and Bodbe in the same trip. For Kazbegi + Mtskheta in one day the schedule is very tight — the guide will advise the best option for your interests.
