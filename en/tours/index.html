Frequently Asked Questions

How much do private tours in Tbilisi cost?
Prices range from ₾90 per person (Mtskheta) to ₾700 (3-day Slow Travel). The most popular tours are Kazbegi — ₾175/person, Kakheti — ₾170/person. The price includes transport and the guide. Entrance tickets are paid separately.
Why choose a private guide over a group tour?
A private guide adapts the route to you: you decide the departure time, pace, and stops. Groups of up to 7 people — no crowds or waiting. Guide Timur will show you places that standard group tours never visit.
Which tours from Tbilisi are the most popular?
Top 3: Kazbegi (mountains, Gergeti Trinity Church, Georgian Military Highway), Kakheti (wine, monasteries, Sighnaghi) and Kutaisi (courtyard houses, sulfur baths, Narikala). All are 1-day trips departing from Tbilisi.
How do I book a tour?
Send a message via WhatsApp or Telegram — guide Timur will reply within 15 minutes. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour starts.
What language are the tours conducted in?
All tours are conducted in English. Guide Timur is fluent in English, lives in Tbilisi, and knows the city inside out.
What is included in the tour price?
Included: private guide, transport (for out-of-town tours). Entrance tickets are paid separately (~₾3–5 per site). Food and drinks are at your own expense, except for the dinner tour (₾250) and wine tasting in Kakheti.
