Walking Tours in Tbilisi — FAQ

What walking tours are available in Tbilisi?
4 routes: Kutaisi walking tour, Night Tbilisi, Soviet architecture, and Digital Nomad Tour. All are conducted on foot with a private guide, groups up to 7 people.
How long do walking tours last?
2-4 hours depending on the route. Night Tbilisi is 2-3 hours, all others are 3-4 hours. The pace is comfortable, with plenty of stops at interesting points along the way.
Do I need to be physically fit?
No, all routes are comfortable. The guide adjusts the pace to your group and makes stops. Comfortable shoes are all you need.
Are the tours suitable for children?
Yes, all walking tours are suitable for children aged 6 and up. The guide adapts the commentary and pace for families.
