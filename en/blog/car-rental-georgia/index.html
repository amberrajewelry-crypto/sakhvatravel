Honest take from a local guide Renting a car is the best way to explore Georgia beyond Tbilisi — but only if you prepare. This guide covers everything: documents, traffic rules, fuel, mountain roads, and when a guided tour is actually the smarter choice.

Required Documents

  • International Driving Permit (IDP) — mandatory for all foreign nationals. Get it before you travel.
  • Passport — for contract signing
  • Credit card (Visa/Mastercard) — for the ₾500–1,000 security deposit block
  • Minimum age — 21–23 years depending on company. Drivers 21–25 pay a surcharge.

Gas stations (Wissol, Socar, Gulf, Lukoil) are on all main highways. No gas stations in mountain areas (Tusheti, Upper Svaneti) — fill up in the last town.

Roads & Routes

Paved highways (any car)

  • Tbilisi — Batumi (360 km, ~5 h) — motorway, good asphalt
  • Tbilisi — Kutaisi (230 km, ~3 h) — best road in the country
  • Tbilisi — Kazbegi (160 km, ~3 h) — Georgian Military Highway. Paved, scenic, mountain pass at 2,379 m
  • Tbilisi — Kakheti (90–120 km, ~1.5–2 h) — good road via Gombori Pass

4WD required

  • Tusheti (Omalo) — one of the world's most dangerous mountain roads. Dirt track, cliff edges. June–October only.
  • Upper Svaneti (Ushguli) — 45 km of dirt road from Mestia, river crossings
  • Goderdzi Pass (Adjara) — partially paved, 4WD recommended after rain
  • Khevsureti (Shatili) — dirt road, 4WD mandatory. June–October
Tip: Check your rental contract for off-road restrictions. Some companies void the insurance if you drive on unpaved roads. On Localrent, you can filter for "off-road allowed".

Renting in Batumi

Batumi is the second most popular city for car rental. Prices are 5–10% higher than Tbilisi. The airport is small — renting in the city or via is better value.

Insurance & Deposit

  • Basic (TPL) — included. Covers third-party damage only.
  • Full (CDW) — ₾20–50/day. Covers the rental car. Strongly recommended for mountain roads.
  • No deposit — available on with full insurance.
  • Green Card — only needed for cross-border trips (Armenia, Turkey). From ₾30/15 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an IDP to rent in Georgia?+
Yes. All companies require a valid International Driving Permit alongside your national licence.
How much does fuel cost?+
AI-95 petrol: ₾3.2–3.5/L ($1.2–1.3). Diesel: ₾3.0–3.3/L. LPG: ₾1.5–1.8/L. No gas stations in Tusheti or Upper Svaneti.
Which roads need 4WD?+
Mandatory: Tusheti (Omalo), Upper Svaneti (Ushguli), Khevsureti (Shatili). Recommended: Goderdzi Pass after rain. All main highways work with any car.
Can I drive to Armenia?+
Only with written permission and extra fee (₾50–150). You'll need Green Card insurance. Not all companies allow it.
Should I get full insurance?+
Yes. Basic covers only third-party damage. Full CDW (₾20–50/day) covers the car itself. On Localrent, full insurance unlocks no-deposit rentals.
Are there speed cameras?+
Yes, on all major roads. Fines are sent to the rental company and charged to your card. ₾40–500 depending on how much you exceed.
