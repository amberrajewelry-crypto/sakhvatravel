Kazbegi (Stepantsminda) — a mountain village 157 km from Tbilisi, at the foot of Mt. Kazbek (5,047 m). The iconic Gergeti Trinity Church (14th century) perches at 2,170 m above the village with a view of the entire Caucasus range. A guided tour from Tbilisi costs from ₾175 per person and takes 10–12 hours. One of the most spectacular day trips in the Caucasus. Don't forget to get travel insurance — mandatory for entering Georgia since 2026.

Why Kazbegi is a must-visit

Kazbegi is the image that defines Georgia in the world's imagination — a medieval church on a cliff against snow-capped peaks, framed by dramatic gorges. It is both the symbol of Georgian Christianity and a gateway to the Greater Caucasus.

What makes Kazbegi special is not just the destination but the journey. The Georgian Military Highway — 212 km of mountain road — passes through Ananuri fortress, Gudauri ski resort and the 2,395 m Jvari Pass. The drive alone is worth the trip.

Kazbegi pairs naturally with a Kakheti wine tour on a multi-day itinerary through Georgia. More on the route: Kazbegi day tour from Tbilisi.

Routes: 1 day, 1.5 days, 2-3 days

Classic 1-day tour (most popular)

Depart Tbilisi at 8:30 → stops along the Military Highway → Stepantsminda village → Gergeti Trinity Church → return by 19:00–20:00. Includes Ananuri Fortress and Gudauri viewpoint. Duration: 10–12 hours. Price from ₾175/person.

Kazbegi + Truso Gorge (1.5 days)

Add the Truso mineral spring gorge east of Stepantsminda — turquoise terraces of calcium carbonate, abandoned villages and fortress ruins. Overnight in Stepantsminda (guesthouses from ₾40/person). Best in July–September when the road is passable.

Kazbegi + Kakheti (2-3 days)

Mountains and wine in one trip: Day 1 — Kazbegi, overnight in Stepantsminda; Day 2 — Truso or rest day; Day 3 — drive back via Kakheti (Sighnaghi, wineries). Price from ₾320/person for the full package.

Georgian Military Highway stops

The Military Highway (E117) connects Tbilisi to Vladikavkaz through the Caucasus. Key stops on the way to Kazbegi:

  • Zhinvali Reservoir (km 60) — turquoise artificial lake framed by forested mountains. Scenic viewpoint, 10-minute stop
  • Ananuri Fortress (km 75) — 17th-century riverside castle with two towers and a frescoed church. Entry ₾3. Allow 30–40 minutes
  • Pasanauri (km 98) — crossroads village famous for khinkali with lamb. Lunch stop on guided tours
  • Gudauri (km 120, 2,200 m) — Georgia's main ski resort. Panoramic viewpoint of the Great Caucasus on a clear day
  • Jvari Pass (2,395 m) — the high point of the road. Snow may be present even in June. Spectacular drop into the Terek river gorge below

Gergeti Trinity Church: jeep or hike?

Gergeti Trinity Church (Tsminda Sameba) sits at 2,170 m on a rocky promontory above the Terek River. Getting there from Stepantsminda village:

  • Shared jeep — ₾30–50 per person return. 4WD vehicles wait in the village square. Journey takes 20 minutes up a rough track
  • Private jeep — ₾100–150 for the whole vehicle. More flexible timing
  • Hiking — 1.5–2 hours up, 1–1.5 hours down. Well-marked trail from the village. Elevation gain ~550 m. Bring water and sun protection — no shade on upper section

The church is an active Orthodox monastery. Entrance is free. Women should cover their heads. Photography inside is restricted. The panoramic view from the churchyard — Mt. Kazbek, the Terek gorge, the village below — is the highlight of the entire trip.

Best time to visit Kazbegi

  • May–June — ideal: green valleys, flowers on the slopes, Gergeti Church often snow-free. Road open. Cool temperatures (10–18°C in village)
  • July–August — peak season: busy, accommodation pricier. Hot in Tbilisi but pleasant at altitude. Best for Truso gorge
  • September–October — golden autumn: larches turn yellow, fewer tourists, clearest skies for mountain views
  • Winter (December–March) — Kazbegi is accessible if the road is open. Gergeti in snow is magical. Gudauri for skiing on the way up
Tip: the Jvari Pass can close after heavy snowfall even in May. Check road conditions before departure. Guided tours include this check automatically.

Food in Stepantsminda

Stepantsminda has a small but quality food scene that has grown with tourism:

  • Khinkali with lamb — the mountain version, larger and more robust than Tbilisi-style. Best eaten at local guesthouses. ₾1.50–2 per piece
  • Terek trout — fresh from the river, grilled or pan-fried. A local specialty not found in Tbilisi. ₾18–28 per portion
  • Mchadi with sulguni — cornbread with Georgian cheese. Hearty mountain breakfast. ₾5–8
  • Guesthouse lunches — most guesthouses offer set lunches with soup, mains and homemade wine. ₾20–30 per person

Practical tips

  • Wear layers — temperature in Stepantsminda is 10–15°C cooler than Tbilisi
  • Bring cash — ATMs in Stepantsminda are limited, card payments not always available
  • Start early — the highway gets busier after 10:00, and afternoon clouds often obscure mountain views
  • Book accommodation in advance in July–August — guesthouses fill up fast
  • Altitude note — Stepantsminda is at 1,740 m, Jvari Pass at 2,395 m. Take it easy if you're sensitive to altitude
