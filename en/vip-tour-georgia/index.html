How the VIP tour works

It starts with a brief: you tell us the dates, group size, occasion and wishes — from cuisine to the pace of the route. Within 5–7 days we agree the programme, choose the car (a business-class sedan, a minivan or an SUV for mountain roads) and, if needed, add a helicopter transfer, personal security and a photographer. A 10% deposit secures a one-day programme (the balance is paid on the spot); multi-day programmes are prepaid 100% before the start.

On the day, guide Timur or Saba meets you at the airport or hotel and leads a private route with no groups or queues: closed tastings with winemakers, a table at the best restaurants, access to sites outside opening hours. Prices start at ₾1000 per person per day and depend on the services chosen; for a party of 2–4 it works out cheaper than ready-made multi-day packages.

You can travel year-round: mountains and sea in summer, the wine and harvest season in Kakheti in September–October, Gudauri and thermal springs in winter. We build the route around the weather and your interests.

FAQ

Can it be a surprise?

Yes. Tell us the occasion when booking.

If the flight is delayed?

The assistant tracks your flight; the welcome adjusts.

How far ahead to book?

Ceremony, security and helicopter need 5–7 days.

Is one day enough?

Yes. A private route with no groups is denser than multi-day packages.

What's included in the price?

The guide, car with driver, fuel, entrance tickets and bookings along the route. Accommodation, flights and restaurants are your choice — paid separately or included in the quote.

Which languages?

Russian and English, fluently. Timur and Saba accompany you in person for the whole route.

Can we bring children?

Yes. We provide child seats and adjust the pace and stops by age — a family VIP format is a common request.
