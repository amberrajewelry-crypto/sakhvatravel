Georgia Travel FAQ

Short answers to what travelers ask most before their trip: visa, money, connectivity, how tours are organized, and safety. Can't find your question — message me on WhatsApp and I'll reply within 15 minutes.

Visa & Entry
Do I need a visa for Georgia?
Most nationalities — including the EU, UK, US and many others — can enter Georgia visa-free for up to 365 days with a valid passport. Check your country on the official entry list before traveling. Full details in our guide: Georgia Visa 2026.
How long can you stay in Georgia without a visa?
Up to one year (365 days) continuously for visa-free nationalities. The period resets after you exit and re-enter the country.
Money & Payments
What currency should I bring to Georgia?
The national currency is the Georgian lari (GEL, ₾). US dollars and euros are easily exchanged at no-commission booths across the city. For the best rate, bring dollars or euros in cash. See the full budget breakdown: How much a Georgia trip costs.
Can I pay by card in Georgia?
Yes. Visa and Mastercard are accepted almost everywhere. For tours, cash or bank transfer is usually easier — we agree on the details over WhatsApp when you book.
Connectivity
How do I buy a SIM card in Georgia?
Operators Magti, Silknet and Cellfie sell tourist SIMs at the airport and in phone shops. You need a passport; a data package costs about 10–25 GEL and takes 5 minutes. Handy for remote workers too — see Digital Nomad in Tbilisi.
Tours & Organization
What languages are the tours conducted in?
Guide Timur runs tours in English and Russian. The itinerary is fully tailored to your group — pace, focus and stops.
Do I need to book an excursion in advance?
Yes, booking 1–2 days ahead is recommended to secure your date and time. In high season, popular routes like Kazbegi and Kakheti should be booked earlier. Browse all tours in the excursions catalog.
Are there age or fitness restrictions?
No. The route adapts to the group: there are relaxed tours for families with kids and seniors, and active ones with trekking. Just share your preferences when booking.
Can I cancel or reschedule a tour?
Yes. Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 48 hours before the tour starts. 10% deposit at booking — the balance is paid on the day of the excursion.

When to go & safety
When is the best time to visit Georgia?
Georgia is great year-round. May–June and September–October offer the best balance of weather and prices. Summer is for the Batumi seaside and Kazbegi mountains; winter for skiing in Gudauri and festive Tbilisi. Season-by-season breakdown: Best time to visit Georgia.
Is Tbilisi safe, including at night?
Yes. Tbilisi is one of the safest cities in the region — you can walk the center comfortably even at night. Locals are friendly to tourists and street crime is low.
