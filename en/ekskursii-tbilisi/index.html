Frequently Asked Questions About Tbilisi Tours

How much do private tours in Tbilisi cost in 2026?
Private tour prices start from ₾77 per person (Mtskheta, 3–4 hours) and go up to ₾595 for the three-day Slow Travel package with accommodation. Day trips from Tbilisi: Kazbegi — from ₾175/person, Kakheti — from ₾170/person, Borjomi — from ₾178/person. All prices include transport and entrance tickets.
How are private tours different from group tours?
The key difference is flexibility. Private tours are not tied to a fixed schedule — you choose the start time, pace, and how long to linger at each stop. With groups of up to 7 people you get genuine personal contact with the guide. Routes include exclusive local spots not found on standard group tour itineraries.
Which day trips from Tbilisi are the most popular?
Top day trips from Tbilisi: 1) Kazbegi — mountains, Gergeti Trinity Church, Georgian Military Highway (10–12 hours); 2) Kakheti — wine, Sighnaghi, Bodbe Monastery (10–12 hours); 3) Mtskheta — UNESCO sites, Svetitskhoveli (3–4 hours). For sea lovers: Batumi from Tbilisi (15 hours).
In what language are the tours conducted?
All our private tours are conducted in English. Guide Timur speaks fluent English and has lived in Tbilisi since 2023. He does not just know the routes — he lives here and shares the city from the inside.
What is included in the tour price?
All tours include: private guide service, transport (comfortable minivan for out-of-city routes), entrance tickets to museums and fortresses. Food and drinks are at your own expense, except for the Georgian family dinner tour (₾213) and wine tasting in Kakheti.
Can I cancel a tour?
Yes. All tours can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before departure — no questions asked, no penalty. Cancellations within 48 hours incur a 50% fee. Rescheduling is always possible — just let us know.
Are there tours for expats and digital nomads?
Yes, the Digital Nomad & Expat Tour is designed specifically for people who have just moved to Tbilisi or are planning to: co-working spaces, best neighbourhoods to rent in, banks, SIM cards, and the best Wi-Fi cafes. A private tour to get oriented fast.
How many people can join a tour?
Minimum 1 person, maximum 7. For groups larger than 7 we can arrange parallel groups or discuss special terms — just message us. Groups of 6–7 get a 10% discount.
