Why hike with a guide

In the mountains the weather turns in minutes, trails aren't always marked and the road to the trailhead can need a 4x4. A licensed guide takes all of that off your plate: he chooses a safe route for your level, watches the weather, sorts the transfer and turns a walk into a story about the Caucasus. You just walk and enjoy the views. Doing the Kazbegi area? See our Kazbegi day trip from Tbilisi too.

Timur's tip for your first mountain hike

Don't overrate the difficulty, but don't underrate the mountains either. For a first hike the Gergeti church or Juta is plenty — beautiful and not exhausting. Wear proper shoes with grip, take a warm layer (it's cold at altitude even in July) and drink water often. We start early to be back in the valley before an afternoon thunderstorm — that's the usual mountain weather here.

Which hike to choose for your level

You don't need mountain experience to hike in Georgia — you need the right route. For a first hike the climb to the Gergeti Trinity Church is ideal: a couple of hours uphill above Stepantsminda, and Mount Kazbek opens up in front of you. If you want a full day among the peaks without technical climbing, Juta and the Chaukhi valley is the answer — alpine meadows, a river and the sharp Chaukhi wall. The Truso valley is an easy but long walk past mineral springs and old towers. And for the fit there's the route toward the Gergeti glacier, where it already feels like high mountains. Timur helps you pick honestly, so the day is a joy and not a struggle.

How a one-day hike from Tbilisi goes

We leave Tbilisi early and drive north along the famous Georgian Military Road — about three hours with stops at the Ananuri fortress, the Jinvali reservoir and the Russia–Georgia Friendship monument with its view of the Devil's Valley. By late morning we reach Stepantsminda (Kazbegi), the base for the mountain routes. Then comes the hike itself — from two to six hours depending on the trail — with breaks for photos and rest. In the evening we drive back to Tbilisi. The whole day takes 10–12 hours, and you return with the Caucasus in your legs and your camera.

When to hike in the mountains of Georgia

June–September. The main hiking season. The passes are clear of snow, the meadows are in flower, all routes — Juta, Truso, the glacier — are open. The best time for the mountains.

May and October. Shoulder season: fewer people, crisp air, but snow can still linger high up. Great for the Gergeti church and easier trails.

November–April. The high routes close, but a winter trip to Kazbegi with snowy peaks and the Gergeti church in the snow is spectacular. We swap trekking for a scenic drive and short walks.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need hiking experience?

No. We pick a route to match your level — from an easy walk to the Gergeti church to a full-day trek in Juta or Truso. Just tell us your fitness and Timur builds the day around it.

Which mountains do you hike?

Most often the Kazbegi area (Gergeti, Juta, Truso valley) — the most accessible from Tbilisi in a day. On a multi-day trip we also go to Svaneti and the Tusheti trails.

What should I bring for the hike?

Comfortable trainers or trekking shoes, a light windbreaker, water and sunscreen. Weather changes fast in the mountains, so a warm layer is worth it even in summer. Timur sends a full checklist before the trip.

Is a one-day hike from Tbilisi possible?

Yes. We leave early, drive to Kazbegi (about 3 hours), hike during the day and return in the evening. For deeper routes in Svaneti we recommend two or three days.

How much does a hike in the mountains of Georgia cost?
