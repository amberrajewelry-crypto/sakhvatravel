Three Practical Tips

  • If you arrive after 23:00, mention it when booking — some parts of the Old Town are closed to traffic after 22:00 (pedestrian zones). Timur knows alternative routes and drop-off points near pedestrian streets.
  • If you have more than 4 large suitcases, let us know when booking. A Toyota Camry fits 2 large bags + carry-on. Larger groups or more luggage require a minivan.
  • When leaving the Old Town, Bolt frequently runs late — drivers won't enter narrow dead-end alleys. Timur's transfer arrives on time to your exact door.

What to Bring

  • A printed or offline-saved copy of your hotel address (in Georgian script — useful if you need to show a security guard)
  • Some Georgian lari in cash — there is an exchange at the airport, though the rate is slightly worse than in the city
  • Your charger in carry-on — Georgia uses 220V with European (type C/F) plugs
  • Hotel booking confirmation — Georgian border control sometimes asks for it
  • Timur's number saved in your phone in advance: +995511272623

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price for the airport transfer in Tbilisi?

Airport transfer from TBS to the city center starts from ₾90 for a sedan (up to 3 passengers). Minivan for up to 7 passengers costs ₾75. Night surcharge +₾10 for arrivals between 00:00–06:00. Exact price depends on your destination — confirm at booking.

How does the driver find me at the airport?

The driver waits in the arrivals hall with a sign bearing your name. You don't need to call or navigate — just walk out of customs and look for the sign. The driver monitors your flight arrival in real time and waits up to 60 minutes after landing.

Is the transfer available 24 hours a day?

Yes, 24/7. For night arrivals (00:00–06:00) a ₾10 surcharge applies. Share your flight number when booking so the driver can track it and adjust arrival time automatically if delayed.

Do you offer a return transfer from the hotel to the airport?

Yes. Return transfer from hotel to TBS airport starts from ₾50, or ₾65 for early morning departures. Timur recommends allowing 2.5–3 hours before your international flight — and 3–3.5 hours during peak holiday periods when security lines are longer.
