What to See

Dadiani Palace is a museum with a rich collection: weapons, jewellery, manuscripts. The botanical garden next to the palace is one of the oldest in the Caucasus, founded in 1840.

On the way you can also visit Martvili Canyon — turquoise water, a boat trip, and waterfalls. Or the 7th-century Martvili Monastery.

Combining with Other Tours

Zugdidi pairs perfectly with a trip to Svaneti (Mestia is another 3 hours further). If time allows, a 2-day route is recommended: Zugdidi + Mestia.

Detailed Route and What to See

The Zugdidi tour begins with an early morning departure from Tbilisi. On the way you have the opportunity to enjoy Georgia's picturesque landscapes, passing through scenic villages and mountain areas. The first stop is Dadiani Palace, the former residence of the Mingrelian princes. Here you can see a unique collection including works of art, weapons, and even Napoleon's death mask — making this place truly extraordinary.

After visiting the palace you proceed to the botanical garden just nearby. Founded in 1840, this garden is one of the oldest in the Caucasus and is renowned for its rare plants and scenic pathways. A walk through the garden offers moments of calm and an opportunity to enjoy the atmosphere of nature. To conclude the tour you can choose an optional stop at Martvili Canyon, where an exciting boat trip along turquoise waters awaits you along with spectacular waterfalls — or visit the historic 7th-century Martvili Monastery, adding a spiritual dimension to your journey.

Throughout the day your guide will share fascinating facts and stories about each place, making your tour even more educational. Don't forget to bring a camera to capture the unforgettable moments of your journey through this remarkable region of Georgia.

Practical Tips

When planning the Zugdidi tour, comfortable shoes and clothing are the key to an enjoyable experience. Since the tour involves a lot of walking, comfortable trainers or shoes are ideal, along with light clothing suited to a warm climate. Depending on the season, a light jacket or sweater may be needed in the evening when temperatures can drop.

  • Bring water and a snack to maintain your energy level throughout the tour.
  • If you plan to take photos, bring a camera or smartphone with a good lens — there are many scenic spots here.
  • Sunscreen and a hat are a must in summer when the sun can be very strong.
  • A small backpack for personal items is recommended for comfortable carrying of essentials.

Timur's Insider Tip

If you are heading to Zugdidi, I recommend departing no later than 7:30 am — the drive from Tbilisi takes about 4.5 hours, and you will arrive with the palace already open (it opens at 10:00). At the museum entrance, take the audio guide — it includes the story of Napoleon and how his death mask ended up here. Don't skip the botanical garden: most tourists miss it, but inside there are century-old sequoias and quiet pathways that exist nowhere else in Georgia. If you have time, the Martvili Canyon boat ride costs ₾10 — take it without hesitation. The water is emerald green and the canyon walls are 40 metres high, giving the feeling of stepping into another world.

Zugdidi is one of the most underrated points in Georgia among English-speaking tourists. Mingrelian food is different here: Mingrelian khachapuri with cheese inside and out, chicken in walnut sauce, and adjika on every table. Visit the Zugdidi market — a small covered market in the city centre where vendors sell homemade suluguni cheese and Mingrelian spices. Smoked suluguni in vacuum packaging travels well back to Tbilisi — it's the best edible souvenir from the region. Market price is around ₾8–12 per kilogram, three times cheaper than Tbilisi shops.
