Tour Programme

Morning: early departure from Tbilisi (~06:30). Short stop in Gori — Goristsikhe fortress, and the Uplistsikhe cave town if you wish. Then Kutaisi: Bagrati Cathedral or Gelati Monastery and an Imeretian lunch.

Daytime: arrival in Batumi around midday. Batumi Boulevard (7 km along the sea), Piazza Square, the Alphabet Tower, the Old Town, and in summer time for a swim in the Black Sea.

Evening: dinner on the seafront and the drive back to Tbilisi along the same route. Arrival late in the evening (~23:30).

Best Time to Visit Batumi

The best time is June–September, when the water warms to +25°C. May and October are also lovely but too cool for swimming. In winter Batumi is beautiful and uncrowded — just don't expect the beach.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive from Tbilisi to Batumi?

Pure driving time is about 5–5.5 hours one way (≈370 km). On this day tour we add short stops in Gori and Kutaisi, so we reach Batumi around midday and return along the same road in the evening.

What stops do we make along the way?

Heading west from Tbilisi we stop in Gori (Goristsikhe fortress, and the Uplistsikhe cave town nearby if you wish) and in Kutaisi (Bagrati Cathedral or Gelati Monastery, plus lunch). The stops are short — stretch your legs, coffee and photos — so there is still time for the sea in Batumi.

What will we see in Batumi?

Batumi Boulevard (7 km along the sea), Piazza Square, the Alphabet Tower with its panoramic lift, the promenade and the Old Town. In summer there is time for a swim in the Black Sea.

Is it a tiring day because of the drive?

Yes, it is a long day: early departure (~06:30) and a late return to Tbilisi — about 740 km round-trip in total. We travel in a comfortable air-conditioned car and make stops. If you prefer a relaxed pace with an overnight stay by the sea, there is a 2-day Batumi tour option.

How much are the entrance fees on the route?

Goristsikhe fortress and the walk along Batumi Boulevard and the beach are free. Entrance to Batumi Botanical Garden is ₾15; Gonio Fortress and the Kutaisi monasteries are paid separately on site in lari.

About Getting to Batumi

Batumi is Georgia's seaside capital on the Black Sea. By plane from Tbilisi: 40 minutes (from ₾30 on Georgian Airways). By overnight train: 5 hours on the Stadler express (from ₾25, business class ₾40). By car: 5–6 hours via Khashuri and Kutaisi on a good quality road. Batumi has a mild subtropical climate: winters +8–12°C, summers +28–32°C. Best season: May–June (warm without the crowds) and September (the velvet season, sea +24°C). Key landmarks: Piazza Square (modelled on a Venetian square), Batumi Boulevard (7 km of seafront promenade), Botanical Garden (entrance ₾15, 113 ha of tropical plants).
