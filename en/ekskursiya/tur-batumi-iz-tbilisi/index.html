Tour Programme

Day 1: Transfer Tbilisi → Batumi with stops in Gori and Borjomi. Batumi Boulevard, Piazza Square, dinner with a sea view. Free time at the beach in the evening.

Day 2: Gonio Fortress (Roman, 1st century AD), Batumi Botanical Garden — 113 hectares of subtropical plants. Return to Tbilisi.

Best Time to Visit Batumi

The best time is June–September, when the water warms to +25°C. May and October are also lovely but too cool for swimming. In winter Batumi is beautiful and uncrowded — just don't expect the beach.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1. 07:30 — departure from Tbilisi. The Tbilisi–Batumi highway (330 km). 10:00 — stop in Gori for coffee and a look at Goristsikhe fortress on the hill. 12:00 — Borjomi: 30 minutes at the mineral springs in the national park — you can drink directly from the taps, each spring has a distinct mineral taste. 14:30 — arrival in Batumi. Check in to the hotel near the seafront (I recommend the zone between Rustaveli Avenue and the beach — 10 minutes walk to the sea). 16:00 — walk along Batumi Boulevard: 7 km hugging the Black Sea. Ferris wheel at the far end of the promenade, Alphabet Tower (130 m, lift ₾3). 19:30 — dinner at Piazza Square with a view of the fountain and city hall. The square is small but the atmosphere is straight out of an Italian town. I recommend the fish restaurant with a terrace on the second floor.

Day 2. 09:00 — breakfast at the hotel. 10:00 — Gonio Fortress (18 km from Batumi, 15 minutes by car). A Roman fortress from the 1st century AD with 22 towers and a 900-metre perimeter. According to legend, the Apostle Matthew is buried here. Entrance ₾3. 12:00 — Batumi Botanical Garden (5 km from the centre, bus #31 or taxi ₾5). 113 hectares of subtropical plants, a Japanese garden, bamboo grove. Ticket ₾15. 14:30 — lunch in Batumi with traditional Adjarian food: Adjarian khachapuri (flatbread boat with egg and butter), or muta (aubergine with walnuts). 16:00 — departure for Tbilisi. Arrival around 21:00–22:00.

Practical Tips for This Tour

  • Book your hotel in the Batumi old quarter (around Ninoshvili Avenue) — prices ₾50–80/night, quiet neighbourhood, 10 minutes walk to the boulevard.

  • The beach in Batumi is pebble, not sand — bring beach shoes or aqua shoes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive from Tbilisi to Batumi?

By car it takes about 5–5.5 hours. This tour includes scenic stops along the way, so you arrive in Batumi roughly 7 hours after departure from Tbilisi.

What will we see in Batumi in one day?

Batumi Boulevard, Piazza Square, the Alphabet Tower with its panoramic lift, the Black Sea promenade, and the Old Town — these are the key highlights of the city tour on Day 2.

When is the best time to visit Batumi?

June–September is swimming season with warm sea (+25°C). May and October offer fewer crowds and pleasant weather for exploring the city on foot. Winter is quiet and beautiful but too cold for the beach.

Is accommodation included in the Batumi tour?

Yes — one night in a comfortable 3-star hotel near the Batumi seafront is included in the price from ₾250.

What will we see in Batumi overall?

Batumi Boulevard, Black Sea promenade, Piazza Square, Gonio-Apsaros Fortress (1st century AD), Batumi Botanical Garden, and the old town with its fascinating blend of European and Eastern architecture.

About Getting to Batumi

Batumi is Georgia's seaside capital on the Black Sea. By plane from Tbilisi: 40 minutes (from ₾30 on Georgian Airways). By overnight train: 5 hours on the Stadler express (from ₾25, business class ₾40). By car: 5–6 hours via Khashuri and Kutaisi on a good quality road. Batumi has a mild subtropical climate: winters +8–12°C, summers +28–32°C. Best season: May–June (warm without the crowds) and September (the velvet season, sea +24°C). Key landmarks: Piazza Square (modelled on a Venetian square), Batumi Boulevard (7 km of seafront promenade), Botanical Garden (entrance ₾15, 113 ha of tropical plants).
