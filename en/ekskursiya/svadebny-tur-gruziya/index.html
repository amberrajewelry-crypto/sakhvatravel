How the Wedding Day Works

Civil registration at the Civil Registry Agency takes 30 minutes. You receive your Georgian marriage certificate the same day. The government provides witnesses — you do not need to bring your own. State fee: ₾50 (express same-day: ₾150).

After registration, the ceremony moves to your chosen location: Ananuri Castle, the Kakheti vineyards, or a panoramic terrace above Tbilisi. A professional photographer captures every moment. The wedding dinner is a genuine Georgian feast — 15–20 dishes, wine from clay amphorae, a tamada leading heartfelt toasts, and live music that turns every table into a celebration.

Best Ceremony Locations in Georgia

Three iconic settings — each with its own atmosphere:

  • Ananuri Castle (75 km from Tbilisi, 1 hour). Medieval towers rising above an emerald reservoir. The castle grounds are open to visitors; entry ₾5. The best shot: through the arched gateway with water in the background.
  • Tsinandali Vineyards, Kakheti (100 km, 1.5 hours). The 19th-century estate of the Chavchavadze princes, with a 14-hectare park. Venue rental available on request. Golden autumn in October is the most photogenic season.
  • Tbilisi Panoramic Terrace — several restaurants in the Mtatsminda district at 700 m altitude with a full panorama of the city. Ideal for an urban wedding with a dramatic backdrop.

Registration Documents — What You Need

Georgia makes international civil marriage refreshingly simple. Required documents:

  • Valid passports of both partners (originals)
  • Divorce certificates if either partner was previously married (originals + Georgian translation)
  • Application form (completed on-site at the Civil Registry)

The Georgian marriage certificate is legally valid in most countries. For use in the UK, US, Canada, and EU, an apostille is required (₾80, issued the same day at the Justice House). Timur helps coordinate the paperwork and appointments in advance so there are no surprises on the day.

Specific Tips for a Wedding in Georgia

  • Book the registration for Wednesday or Thursday — fewer queues at the Civil Registry Agency. Fridays and Saturdays can have waits of 3–5 couples ahead of you.
  • The Georgian marriage certificate is legally recognized in the UK, EU, US, and most other countries with an apostille (₾80, same-day processing).
  • Best months for photography: April (spring blossoms), May (green hills with snow on the mountains simultaneously), October (golden autumn in Kakheti vineyards).
  • For Sighnaghi ceremonies — the "City of Love" — registration is available around the clock. You need passports, Georgian-language translations (notarized), and a certificate of civil status. Venue from ₾500 including the hall. Book at least one month in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners legally get married in Georgia?

Yes. Georgia officially registers civil marriages of foreign nationals at the Civil Registry Agency. The process takes one day: valid passports are the only requirement. The Georgian marriage certificate is internationally recognized. An apostille (₾80) is required for use in most Western countries and takes the same day to process.

Where in Georgia can a wedding ceremony be held?

Medieval churches of Mtskheta, Ananuri Castle, the vineyards of Kakheti, the mountains of Kazbegi — the guide helps you choose and organize the ceremony of your dreams. For something unconventional: rooftop restaurants in Tbilisi old town, private villa estates in Kakheti, or the round-the-clock registry office in Sighnaghi, the "City of Love."

What does a wedding tour in Georgia include?

Ceremony organization, coordination of the Civil Registry appointment, photo session at the best locations, banquet at a traditional Georgian restaurant, full-day private transfer, and document assistance. The complete program is assembled individually based on your preferences and budget.

Can we have a religious or symbolic ceremony?

Orthodox Christian ceremonies are available at Georgian churches with advance arrangement. For other faiths, symbolic ceremonies at any scenic outdoor location are the most popular option — no legal paperwork required, maximum creative freedom.

What is the best time of year for a wedding in Georgia?

April (spring blossoms), May (green mountains with snow simultaneously), September (warm, clear, uncrowded), and October (golden Kakheti harvest season) are the best months for outdoor ceremonies and photography. Avoid July–August when Tbilisi regularly reaches 36–38°C.
