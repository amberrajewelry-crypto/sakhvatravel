Detailed Day-by-Day Programme

Days 1–2: Tbilisi and Mtskheta. Old Tbilisi, Narikala fortress, Abanotubani sulphur bath district, Metekhi Church, Jvari Monastery, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, Georgian food tour.

Days 3–4: Kazbegi. Georgian Military Highway, Ananuri fortress, Gudauri ski resort, Jvari Pass (2,395 m). Gergeti Trinity Church hike. Overnight in the mountains.

Day 5: Kakheti. Sighnaghi — the "City of Love", Bodbe Monastery, qvevri wine tasting at a family winery.

Day 6: Batumi or Svaneti (your choice). Flight or road transfer.

Day 7: Free day, souvenirs, airport transfer.

Flexible Itinerary

Timur adapts the programme to your interests — more wine, more mountains, more sea. You decide the emphasis, and the route adjusts accordingly. This is a private tour, not a group bus.

Detailed Programme with Times

Day 1 — Tbilisi. 15:00 — airport pickup. Check-in at Old Town hotel. 17:00 — first walk: Bridge of Peace, Rike Park, Mtkvari riverfront. 19:30 — dinner in Old Tbilisi: khinkali dumplings and Saperavi wine. Introduction to Georgian feast culture (supra).

Day 2 — Mtskheta + Tbilisi. 10:00 — Mtskheta (20 km from Tbilisi, 25 minutes). Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (11th century), Jvari Monastery (6th century, views over the Aragvi–Mtkvari confluence). 13:00 — back to Tbilisi. Tour: Narikala, Abanotubani sulphur baths, Metekhi quarter. Evening free.

Day 3 — Kazbegi. 08:00 — departure. Ananuri (75 km), fortress above the reservoir. Gudauri ski resort at 2,200 m. Jvari Pass (2,395 m). 14:00 — Stepantsminda. Hike to Gergeti Trinity Church (2,170 m): 1.5 hours on foot or 4WD taxi (~₾20/person). Night at a mountain guesthouse.

Day 4 — Kazbegi to Tbilisi. Morning light on Mount Kazbek (5,047 m) — best photography before 09:00. 11:00 — drive back to Tbilisi. Evening: gastro walk through Old Town — spice shops, churchkhela, shoti bread bakery.

Day 5 — Kakheti. 09:00 — departure via Gombori Pass (1,600 m). Telavi — Kakheti's capital. Tsinandali Palace (19th century, first European-style park in Georgia). Sighnaghi — "the City of Love", 4 km of fortress walls. Family winery: qvevri clay amphoras buried in the ground — an 8,000-year-old method. Tasting of 4–5 wines.

Day 6 — Batumi or Svaneti. Batumi: 1-hour flight (~₾80) or 5-hour drive. Black Sea, Piazza Square, Alphabet Tower (130 m). Svaneti: Mestia, 11th–12th century defensive towers, Chalaati glacier (4-hour trek).

Day 7 — Return. Free morning in Tbilisi. Souvenirs: Deserter's Market (spices 3× cheaper than tourist shops). 15:00–18:00 — airport transfer.

Three Practical Tips for the 7-Day Tour

  • Arrive on the earliest flight of Day 1 — don't waste daylight arriving late at night.
  • Pack a warm jacket for Kazbegi even in July — at 2,170 m near Gergeti it drops to +5°C at night.
  • Kakhetian home wine from qvevri costs ₾8–12 per litre at the winery — the same bottle costs 3× more in city shops.

What to Bring

  • Hiking boots or ankle-support trainers — the Gergeti ascent is on rocky terrain
  • Warm layer (fleece or light down jacket) for Kazbegi and Svaneti
  • Swimwear if you choose Batumi on Day 6
  • Portable power bank — mountain areas have weak signal and drain batteries faster
  • Georgian lari in cash — family wineries in villages often don't accept cards

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the 7-day Georgia tour cover?

Tbilisi, Kazbegi, Kakheti, Gori, Uplistsikhe Cave City, and your choice of Batumi or Svaneti — a complete circuit of Eastern and Western Georgia with overnight stays in each region.

Is a guide included for the full 7 days?

Yes, Timur accompanies the group all 7 days: handling transport, accommodation coordination (on request), sightseeing, and support throughout the entire route.

When is the best time to take the 7-day Georgia tour?

April–June and August–October are optimal: mountain roads are open, the weather allows visiting both Kazbegi and Batumi without extreme heat. Winter is possible and scenic, but Gergeti Church may be inaccessible due to snow.
