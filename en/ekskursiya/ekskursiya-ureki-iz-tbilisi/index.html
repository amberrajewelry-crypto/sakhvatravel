The Magnetic Sands of Ureki

Ureki's defining feature is its black sand, rich in magnetite — it really is magnetic and is widely believed to have healing properties; "sand baths" are a local tradition.

The sand warms in the sun and gently radiates heat, so it is pleasant to lie on, and children love that it is firm rather than loose like ordinary sand.

The Beach and Relaxing

The sea at Ureki is shallow and warm with a gradual entry, which is exactly why families with young children come here. The beach is wide, with umbrellas, loungers and cafes along the shore.

This is a calm resort without Batumi's busy promenade — people come simply for the sea and the sand.

What Else Is Nearby

Near Ureki, the village of Shekvetili has a pine park and an amusement park, and there is the green area of Nasakirali. You can easily combine the beach with a short walk.

For those who want a city, Batumi is not far — Ureki can become part of a longer route through Adjara.

Timur's insider tip On the magnetic sand it is worth trying a "sand bath" at least once — burying yourself in the warm black sand on the shore. Locals swear by it, and it feels good either way. If you are travelling with children, pick a weekday: at weekends Georgian families come to Ureki too and the beach gets noticeably busier. Bring everything you need for the sea — the value here is the long, unhurried day on the sand, not ticking off sights.

Detailed itinerary

Ureki is a long trip to the Black Sea: the drive from Tbilisi takes about 4.5 hours each way, so we leave early and keep most of the day for the beach and swimming.

The plan is flexible: you can spend the whole day on the sand, or add the Shekvetili park or a short stop on the way. On request, Ureki can become a two-day coastal trip with an overnight.

Frequently asked questions

Is the sand at Ureki really therapeutic?

Ureki's sand is rich in magnetite, and locals take "sand baths" believing they help joints and circulation. We make no medical claims, but a day on the warm black sand is certainly pleasant.

Is Ureki good for children?

Yes — it is one of Georgia's best family beaches: a warm shallow sea, a gentle entry and firm sand that does not blow around like ordinary sand.

When is the best time to go?

The swimming season is June to September. Outside it you can still enjoy the sea air, but the water will be too cold for swimming.
