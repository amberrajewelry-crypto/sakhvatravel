Why Summer Is the Best Time

June–August: everything is open. Tusheti, Svaneti, Truso Gorge — all high-altitude routes become accessible in summer. Kazbegi is green, with flowering alpine meadows stretching up to the glacier. Kakheti in July–August features ripening vineyards and early grape varieties ready for tasting.

July and August in Tbilisi are hot (35–38°C), so the best approach to city sightseeing is early mornings and evenings. During the midday heat, the itinerary moves to mountain regions where it's cool and comfortable.

The Summer Route

Days 1–2: Tbilisi. Morning walks (sulfur baths before 10:00 AM), evening excursions (Narikala at sunset). Late-night wine tasting in Old Town cafes.

Day 3: Kazbegi. Green mountains, blooming meadows. Trekking around Gergeti Trinity Church, swimming in a mountain river — the best summer day in Georgia.

Day 4: Kakheti. Vineyards, Sighnaghi, lunch with winemakers. Ртвели (grape harvest) starts in September, but August vineyards are already spectacular.

Days 5–6: Batumi. Black Sea coast, Adjarian cuisine (achma, Adjarian boat-shaped khachapuri). Excursion to mountain Adjaria where it stays cool even in summer.

Return to Tbilisi. Related article: Kazbegi day trip from Tbilisi.

Why Choose the Summer Tour

Summer in Georgia is far more than just heat in Tbilisi (+35°C). The mountains of Kazbegi and Svaneti offer +18–22°C, alpine meadows, and glaciers. Batumi and the Black Sea coast provide beaches and botanical gardens. In Kakheti, the grapes are ripening — and you can participate in ртвели (harvest) in September. I design the summer itinerary to avoid peak heat: mountain mornings, waterside evenings. All transfers are in air-conditioned vehicles.

Timur's Tip

In summer, Tbilisi reaches +35–40°C. Plan mountain routes (Kazbegi, Gudauri, Truso Gorge) for the morning. City sightseeing works best after 17:00 or in the evening. Essentials: water, SPF50+ sunscreen, a hat. In the mountains, temperatures drop — bring a light layer even in July.

Detailed Day-by-Day Plan

Day 1: Tbilisi in the Morning

08:00 — Start in Old Town while the city is still cool. Abanotubani sulfur baths: domed rooftops, steam, morning mist over the Kura River. 09:30 — Climb to Narikala Fortress while the walls are still in shade. 11:00 — Coffee and Georgian cheese on Shardeni Street (~₾12 for breakfast). Midday hours (13:00–16:00) are best spent somewhere cool — a cafe, or the National Museum (entry ₾10).

Day 2: Kazbegi (the Most Popular Summer Route)

07:00 — Early departure from Tbilisi (best timing: drive there before peak heat, return in the evening). 09:30 — Ananuri Fortress, swim in Zhinvali Reservoir (summer water temperature +22°C). 11:00 — Kazbegi, Gergeti Trinity Church. In summer the slopes are vivid green with flowering alpine meadows. Trekking loop around the church — 2.5 km, 45 minutes. Swimming in the Terek mountain river. 17:00 — Departure back to Tbilisi.

Day 3: Kakheti Wine Tasting

09:00 — Departure to Alazani Valley (75 km). 10:30 — Tevisi Marani winery in Telavi: tasting of 5 varieties (₾25/person), cellar tour with qvevri clay vessels. In July, Kakheti is +35°C outside — the wine cellars provide welcome coolness. 14:00 — Lunch with winemakers: grilled mtskvadi, fresh herbs, homemade wine. 16:00 — Sighnaghi town: city walls, panoramic views over the valley.

Days 4–5: Batumi and Adjaria

08:00 — Departure Tbilisi to Batumi (~4.5 hours, 380 km). Batumi boulevard: palm trees, sea, Adjarian khachapuri in a boat shape (~₾15). Mountain Adjaria — Makhutseti: waterfall and Queen Tamara's medieval bridge. In the mountains of Adjaria it stays +22°C even in August. Overnight in Batumi (hostel from ₾45, hotel from ₾90).

Summer Travel Tips — from Timur

  • Departure time: In July–August, leave before 08:00 — roads are quieter and the sun is gentler. After noon in Tbilisi, it's better to stay near water or in a cafe.
  • Water: Bring 2L per person per day. There are springs in Kazbegi and Kakheti but they're not always easy to reach.
  • Kazbegi in shorts: Considered disrespectful for entering Gergeti Church. Pack a long scarf or light trousers.
  • Booking ahead: July–August is peak season. Best hotels in Sighnaghi and Batumi fill up 2–3 weeks in advance. Let Timur know your dates early.

Detailed Route Notes

In summer (+30–35°C in Tbilisi), I plan routes with the heat in mind: mornings in the mountains (Kazbegi, Gudauri — +20°C there), midday near water (Martvili Canyon, Lake Bazaleti — entry ₾2, clear water), evenings in the city (rooftop restaurants, night-time Tbilisi). Best summer destinations: Svaneti (July–August is the only time the Mestia pass is snow-free), Tusheti (open June–September, road via Abano Pass at 2926m). Batumi in summer: beach + nightlife, but very busy. Tip: book 2–3 weeks ahead — in summer I run 2–3 tours per day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it too hot in Tbilisi in summer? Where is better to go?

July–August in Tbilisi reaches +38°C. The guide plans early departures and mountain routes (Kazbegi, Svaneti) — cool, beautiful, and much more comfortable in summer than the city.
