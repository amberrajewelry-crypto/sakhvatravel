Gomis Mta is a mountain peak in Adjara at 2755 metres, from which you can see the Black Sea and the mountains of Turkey at the same time. Machakhela Gorge is a wild valley with waterfalls, medieval stone bridges and villages of just five families. This is not a tourist route — no buses come here. A dirt track through the forest requires a 4WD vehicle.

What makes this tour different

The Batumi city tour shows you the seafront, botanical garden and illuminated tower. Gomis Mta shows you something else entirely: mountains above Batumi, wild untouched nature, and not a single other tourist in sight. These are two completely different worlds — and just 40 minutes separate them.

Timur walks the trail to the waterfall himself and knows a spot where clouds pass below you. On a clear day you can see the Caucasus range on one side and the Black Sea on the other — simultaneously, from a single viewpoint.

Timur's tip

Come in the morning — the mountain is clearest before noon and before the afternoon clouds roll in. If you want to see both Gomis Mta and Batumi in one day, that is possible too: the summit in the morning and the city in the evening. Two different worlds in a single day.

Frequently asked questions

Is any special preparation needed for the Gomis Mta tour?

No special preparation is required, but wear comfortable shoes with a good grip — you will walk on mountain trails. The guide takes care of all logistics.

Where does the tour depart from?

Departure is from Batumi at 08:00. Timur can also pick you up from Kobuleti or Chakvi — just let him know in advance.

How is Gomis Mta different from the Batumi city tour?

The Batumi tour covers the city, seafront, and botanical garden. Gomis Mta is a 2755m mountain above Batumi — wild nature, no tourist crowds, and a viewpoint where clouds pass below you. Two completely different worlds just 40 minutes apart.
