Kura River Boat Walk in Tbilisi — A Sunset Cruise

A boat walk on the Kura (Mtkvari) river is an unusual way to see Tbilisi. From the water you catch views you cannot get from the streets: the Metekhi church on its cliff, the Bridge of Peace, Narikala fortress and the balconies of the Old Town. It is a short city outing of 30 minutes, especially beautiful at sunset, and a perfect add-on to a walking tour.

What You See from the Water

The Kura divides Tbilisi in two, and many of the city's best views face the river. From the boat you get a clear line of sight to the Metekhi church on its cliff, the glass Bridge of Peace, the cable car to Narikala and the dense rows of Old Town balconies.

It is a fresh perspective on a familiar city: what the streets and buildings hide by day, the river reveals all at once.

Sunset Is the Best Time

We schedule the walk for the evening on purpose: at sunset the walls of Narikala and Metekhi glow warm, and then the night lighting of the bridges and embankments comes on. The city visibly changes before your eyes.

For photography this is the best hour in Tbilisi — and from the water the shots are especially striking.

How to Combine It

The Kura boat walk is short and complements a walking route through Old Tbilisi or an evening tour. The natural order is to stroll the city first and meet the sunset on the water.

The format suits couples, families and anyone who wants an unusual experience in a small amount of time.

Timur's insider tip Take the walk at sunset and sit on the right-hand side as the boat sets off — that side has the best line on Narikala and Metekhi in the evening light. My ideal Tbilisi evening goes like this: a stroll through the Old Town, a boat on the Kura at sunset, then dinner with a view of the illuminated fortress. The river ties it all together into one impression.

Detailed itinerary

This is a short city outing, not a day trip: you meet in the centre, spend 30 minutes on the water and return to the same pier. That makes it easy to slot into the evening alongside dinner or a walk through the Old Town.

The route runs along the central stretch of the river, past the key sights of Tbilisi. We set the start time around sunset so you catch both the daytime city and the evening illumination.

  • 17:00Meet at the pier — gathering in central Tbilisi, boarding
  • 17:10Metekhi & the Old Town — views from the water of the church on its cliff and the historic centre
  • 17:30Bridge of Peace — the modern glass bridge — a symbol of new Tbilisi
  • 17:50Narikala & the sulphur baths — panorama of the fortress and the Abanotubani district
  • 18:20Return to the pier — disembark; optionally continue on foot
