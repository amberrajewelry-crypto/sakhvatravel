Full-Day Corporate Program

Morning: guided tour of Old Tbilisi with historical context adapted for a professional audience. Business travelers appreciate concrete facts, statistics, and connections between history and the present day.

Midday: a masterclass in making khachapuri or a guided Georgian wine tasting with expert commentary. Two formats available: a team competition (who kneads the dough best — 5–7 teams of 4–5 people, ₾25/person) or an educational tasting of 6 grape varieties with a sommelier (₾30/person).

Evening: dinner in a historic building with views of the fortress. Live music available on request, traditional Georgian table, toasts. The Georgian feast tradition — the tamada (toastmaster) and the system of toasts — is perfectly suited to a corporate setting.

Why Teams Choose Georgia for Corporate Retreats

  • Authenticity: Georgia is not yet a mass-market corporate destination. Your team experiences something genuinely different from the standard conference tourism circuit.
  • Wine culture: Georgian winemakers are passionate and hospitable. A winery visit becomes a real conversation rather than a scripted tasting performance.
  • Cost: corporate programs in Georgia cost significantly less than equivalent quality programs in Western Europe, with no compromise on experience.
  • Logistics: Tbilisi is well-connected by direct flights from most European cities, visa-free for most nationalities, with excellent hotels across all price ranges.

Detailed Day Schedule

Standard format: 8–10 hours. For groups of 15 or more I coordinate multiple minivans. All stops are reserved in advance — no queues, no surprises.

  • 09:30 — hotel pickup. Brief program overview en route, name badges with Georgian names distributed (optional team building element)
  • 10:00 — Old Tbilisi. Route: Metekhi Bridge → Abanotubani sulphur baths → Narikala Fortress. Historical context focused on the Silk Road through Tbilisi — directly relevant for companies operating in the Caucasus and Central Asia
  • 11:30 — masterclass. Two formats: khachapuri (team competition, 5–7 teams of 4–5 people, ₾25/person) or wine (educational tasting of 6 varieties with a sommelier, ₾30/person)
  • 13:30 — lunch in a private restaurant hall. Traditional Georgian table: pkhali, satsivi, shashlik, khinkali. I arrange a tamada (feast host) — either myself or an invited Georgian who explains the toasting tradition. ₾40–60/person including drinks
  • 15:30 — optional out-of-town program. Options: Mtskheta (20 min) — Georgia's ancient capital, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (12th century); Kakheti (90 min) — winery tasting, evening with live music
  • 18:00 — return to hotel, or transfer to a rooftop restaurant with views of Narikala Fortress (summer terrace)

Three Practical Tips for Organizers

  • Book 2–4 weeks in advance — restaurants with private halls for 15–30 people in Tbilisi fill up quickly, especially May through October
  • Add a chacha ceremony at the end of dinner — 5 minutes, but remembered for a year. I explain the rules of chacha (Georgian home-distilled grape spirit, 60%+): first toast for the hosts, second for the guests
  • For mixed-language teams I provide bilingual accompaniment: RU+EN simultaneously, without delayed interpretation
