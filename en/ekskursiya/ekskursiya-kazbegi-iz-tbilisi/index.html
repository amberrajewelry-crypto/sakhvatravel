Gergeti Trinity Church

The Tsminda Sameba church (XIV century) stands at 2170m on a rocky spur above Stepantsminda village with Mt. Kazbek (5047m) directly behind it. The combination of medieval stone church, green valley, glaciers and a nearly 6000m peak in the frame is the defining image of Georgia.

The church is still active — monks live here year-round. On clear days the summit of Kazbek is snow-covered and perfectly visible. Timur's advice: Kazbek starts gathering clouds by noon, so the 09:00 departure is not arbitrary — it's the key to a clear view.

The Georgian Military Highway

The road from Tbilisi to Kazbegi follows the ancient route across the Caucasus — the same path used by traders, armies, and poets for centuries. Lermontov wrote about Daryal Gorge here, and Pushkin crossed the same pass. The entire drive is spectacular scenery.

Key stops along the way: Zhinvali Reservoir (turquoise water between mountains), Ananuri Fortress (XVI–XVII century, free entry), Pasanauri village (junction with Aragvi rivers), and the Friendship of Nations Arch with its Soviet mosaic murals at 2196m.

Practical Tips

Timur's tip: leave Tbilisi by 09:00 without exception — Kazbek clouds over by noon and the view disappears. At Gergeti, take the jeep (₾20 per person) rather than hiking if you want to maximize time at the top. The best café in Stepantsminda is "Guda" — ask for khinkali with mountain herbs.

  • Dress in layers: it is typically 10–15°C cooler at 2170m than in Tbilisi, even in summer.
  • Comfortable shoes: the path up to Gergeti is unpaved and rocky.
  • Cash in GEL: jeep drivers and mountain cafés are cash-only.
  • Best season: May–October for the best visibility. November–April the pass may be snowy but accessible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Kazbegi tour from Tbilisi?

The tour takes 12 hours total. We depart at 09:00 and return to Tbilisi around 21:00. The route includes Ananuri Fortress, Cross Pass 2395m, Gergeti Trinity Church 2170m, and lunch in Stepantsminda.

Is the Gergeti Trinity Church accessible without a jeep?

Yes — you can hike up in about 1.5 hours each way on a well-marked trail. Alternatively, local jeeps wait at the village square and charge ₾20 per person round trip. We arrange the jeep on site if you prefer not to hike.

What is the best time to visit Kazbegi?

May through October for clear mountain views. We depart at 09:00 specifically because Mt. Kazbek clouds over by noon. Winter visits are possible but the summit view may be obscured.

What is included in the Kazbegi tour price?

Transfer from your Tbilisi hotel and back, private English-speaking guide, all stops including Ananuri Fortress and Cross Pass. Not included: jeep to Gergeti (₾20), lunch (₾20–30), entrance fees.

Timur's Insider Tip

Most tourists photograph Gergeti from the village square — but the real perspective is from the church itself looking down at the village with Kazbek behind you. That requires being there before 11:00, which is exactly why we leave at 09:00. The descent through Daryal Gorge on the way back is different from the morning drive — the light changes and the gorge walls are 1000m high on both sides. We slow down there for photos.
