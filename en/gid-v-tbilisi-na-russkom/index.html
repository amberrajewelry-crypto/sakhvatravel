Maximum 7 people. Most tours run with 2–5 guests. This ensures everyone hears the guide, can ask questions, and can choose the direction of the tour. No microphones, no rushing through crowds.

Yes, free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour. Cancellations within 24 hours incur a 50% fee. Rescheduling is always possible — message the guide and he will sort it out.

Yes — the Digital Nomad & Expat Tour covers everything a newcomer needs: best co-working spaces, neighbourhoods to rent in, Georgian bank options, SIM cards, markets, and cafes where locals actually go. A 3–4 hour orientation that saves weeks of figuring things out on your own.
Where does the tour start?
Timur picks you up at your hotel or meets you at an agreed central point. The meeting location is confirmed when you book. For day trips the pickup is at your accommodation.
