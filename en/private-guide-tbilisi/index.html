Why travel with a local

I'm Timur — I've lived in the Georgian capital since 2019 and have hosted more than 500 trips for visitors from across the world. Travelling with a resident means you skip the tourist traps: you eat where families eat, hear the stories behind every church and fortress, and set the pace yourself instead of following a flag in a crowd.

Every itinerary is built around you. Couples often choose slow walks through the old quarters and sulphur baths; parents with kids prefer a relaxed mini-group; wine lovers head to the Kakheti valleys for cellar tastings and a homemade supra feast. Pick-up is from your hotel, payment is on the day, and you can cancel free of charge 24 hours ahead.

Not sure which route fits your dates? Message me on WhatsApp and I'll suggest the best option for your budget, season and party size — no obligation, just honest advice from someone who drives these mountain roads every week.

Frequently asked questions

What language are the tours in?

Tours run in fluent English, and Russian is also available. Just tell me your preference when you book.

How much does a private tour cost?

Prices are per person, with a 10% discount for groups of 4 or more. Day trips start around €35–45 per person depending on the route and group size — message me for an exact quote.

Do you pick up from my hotel?

Yes — I collect you from your hotel or apartment anywhere in Tbilisi and bring you back at the end of the day.

How do I book and can I cancel?

Booking takes a 10% deposit; you pay the rest on the day. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
