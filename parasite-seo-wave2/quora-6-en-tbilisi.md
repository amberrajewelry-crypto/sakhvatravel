# Quora: What are the best day trips from Tbilisi?

---

**Answer from: Tbilisi Local Guide (Sakhva Travel)**

Tbilisi is an excellent base for day trips — the country is small, the roads are scenic even when slow, and you can reach radically different landscapes and historical eras in two to three hours in any direction.

Here are six options ranked by how often I recommend them, with honest time and cost estimates:

---

## 1. Kazbegi / Mount Kazbek — the classic (9–10 hours)

The Georgian Military Highway north through the Caucasus, stopping at Ananuri fortress, the Gudauri viewpoint, and arriving at Stepantsminda village (still widely called Kazbegi). The target is Gergeti Trinity Church at 2,170 meters with Mount Kazbek (5,047m) behind it on a clear day.

**Non-negotiable rule**: leave Tbilisi before 8 AM. The mountain clouds over by midday most days. Arrive early and you get the photo. Arrive at noon and you get fog.

**Cost**: Private car with driver, ~200–300 GEL. Shared marshrutka, 15 GEL each way (no flexibility).

---

## 2. Mtskheta — the ancient capital (half-day, 3–4 hours)

20 km from Tbilisi. Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (a UNESCO site, the spiritual heart of Georgia), Jvari Monastery on the cliff above the confluence of two rivers, and a very walkable old town with good churchkhela sellers. Easy to combine with another site in the same day.

**Cost**: Taxi round trip ~50–70 GEL, or add it as a stop on a longer trip.

---

## 3. Kakheti wine region (8–10 hours)

Georgia invented wine 8,000 years ago and Kakheti is where most of it is still made. A good day includes Alaverdi Monastery Winery, the hilltop town of Sighnaghi, and a family winery for proper amber wine tasting. You'll be drinking — don't drive yourself.

**Cost**: Organized group tour, 80–120 GEL per person. Private guided tour, 300–450 GEL for the car.

---

## 4. Uplistsikhe cave city + Gori (7–8 hours)

Uplistsikhe is a rock-cut city dating from the 1st century BC, carved into a cliff above the Mtkvari River. Eerie and spectacular. Combine with a stop in Gori (Stalin's birthplace — yes, there's a museum) and the Surami fortress. History layered over history.

**Cost**: Private car with driver, ~180–250 GEL.

---

## 5. Davit Gareja monastery complex (8–9 hours)

A cave monastery carved into desert cliffs on the Azerbaijan border. Founded in the 6th century, with Byzantine-era frescoes still intact. The landscape is completely unlike the rest of Georgia — semi-arid, dramatic, almost otherworldly. Road requires a proper vehicle (4WD recommended).

**Cost**: Private tour with 4WD and guide, ~300–400 GEL. Not well-served by public transport.

---

## 6. Borjomi spa town (6–7 hours)

The Soviet-era resort town known for its mineral water. Good for a slower day: the national park, walking in the forest, drinking water directly from natural springs (the carbonated water really does taste different here). Less dramatic than mountains or monasteries — better if your group includes people who want a relaxed pace.

**Cost**: Train from Tbilisi, ~15 GEL each way. Very comfortable option.
