# Content Quality & E-E-A-T Audit — sakhva-travel.com

**Date:** 2026-05-04
**Auditor:** Claude Opus 4.6 (Content Quality Specialist)
**Site:** https://sakhva-travel.com
**Pages analyzed:** 5 key pages + full-site word count scan (54 blog + 12 tour pages)

---

## Overall Content Quality Score: 78 / 100

Strong first-person E-E-A-T signals, good schema markup, and solid content depth. Main weaknesses: thin tour pages, missing privacy/contact page signals, and EN homepage content gap vs RU.

---

## 1. E-E-A-T Breakdown

### Experience (20% weight) — Score: 85/100

**Strengths:**
- First-person narrative throughout blog articles ("I go to the baths once a month", "I always make my first stop here", "Over 500 tours since 2023")
- Specific local details that cannot be faked: "Khinkali in Pasanauri are better than in Stepantsminda", "come before 10am to avoid tourist buses at Ananuri"
- Review quotes with named reviewers, dates, and source attribution (Google Maps)
- Photo gallery with real tourist photos on homepage
- Guide bio with specific claims (lives in Tbilisi 10+ years, 500+ tourists, rating 4.9)

**Weaknesses:**
- No /about/ page content was verified in this audit (should contain full guide credentials)
- No video testimonials or video content featuring Timur speaking
- Blog articles lack date-stamped photos from actual tours (just OG images)

### Expertise (25% weight) — Score: 72/100

**Strengths:**
- Detailed pricing comparisons (marshrutka vs taxi vs guide) with current 2026 prices
- Seasonal recommendations backed by personal observation ("in May-June Kazbek is visible 7 out of 10 mornings")
- Practical checklists (what to bring, what's included/excluded)
- Internal linking between related content pieces (Kazbegi blog -> Kazbegi tour -> winter Kazbegi article)

**Weaknesses:**
- No formal tourism credentials mentioned (licensed guide badge in footer, but no certificate/association)
- No external citations from authoritative tourism bodies (Georgian National Tourism Administration has a link but not a quote or endorsement)
- article:author meta points to "Timur -- Sakhva Travel" but no standalone author page URL
- Sources block in Kazbegi article has only 3 links (gnta.ge, meteo.ge, mfa.gov.ge) -- could be richer
- Bani article has NO sources block at all

### Authoritativeness (25% weight) — Score: 68/100

**Strengths:**
- Google Business Profile verified (CID 14070083063461040701)
- 87 Google reviews, 4.9 rating (referenced in schema and on page)
- Active WhatsApp/Telegram/Instagram presence (social proof signals)
- llms.txt present (forward-thinking AI citation readiness)

**Weaknesses:**
- No press mentions, media features, or partnership badges
- No TripAdvisor/Viator/GetYourGuide profile links (common authority signal in travel)
- No "as featured in" section
- Instagram link exists but no follower count or engagement proof displayed
- No external backlinks demonstrated on-page (e.g., quoted by other blogs)
- Missing Parasite SEO reverse links from the 22 external publications back to the site

### Trustworthiness (30% weight) — Score: 80/100

**Strengths:**
- Real phone number (+995 511 272 623) in nav, footer, and CTAs
- Email address (help@sakhva-travel.com) in footer
- Physical location stated: "Tbilisi, Georgia / Licensed guide"
- Privacy Policy link (/privacy/)
- Cancellation Policy link (/policy/)
- Free cancellation 24h policy prominently displayed
- "Pay on tour day" -- no upfront payment required (trust signal)
- Google Consent Mode v2 properly implemented
- Cookie bar with consent controls
- HTTPS (via Vercel)
- hreflang correctly implemented (ru/en/x-default)

**Weaknesses:**
- No physical office address (P.O. box or street address)
- No business registration number
- No "Licensed by [Authority]" badge with verifiable number
- Privacy policy and cancellation policy exist but content not verified in this audit
- No SSL badge or security trust marks

## 3. Readability Assessment

Based on Kazbegi blog and Bani blog articles:

- **Sentence length:** Short to medium (15-20 words average). Good.
- **Paragraph length:** 2-4 sentences. Good for mobile.
- **Heading hierarchy:** H1 -> H2 -> H3 properly nested. TOC present.
- **Lists and tables:** Price comparison tables, checklists, route timelines. Strong scanability.
- **Reading level:** Estimated grade 7-8 (accessible to general audience)
- **Bold key terms:** Used consistently for prices, place names, practical info
- **Info boxes / warning boxes:** Used for important notes (dress code, seasonal warnings)

**Readability score: 82/100** -- well-structured for travel audience, good use of formatting elements.

## 4. AI Citation Readiness Score: 76/100

### What works:
- **Quotable lead paragraphs:** Both blog articles have a structured lead with key facts (distance, price, time) that LLMs can extract directly
- **Key fact blocks:** Dedicated "Korotko" (Summary) blocks with structured data at top of articles
- **Price tables:** Structured comparison data ideal for AI extraction
- **FAQ schema:** BlogPosting + FAQPage schema on blog articles with clear Q&A pairs
- **llms.txt file:** Present at /llms.txt (forward-thinking)
- **Clear hierarchy:** H2 -> H3 mapping to intent-based questions ("How to get there?", "What's cheaper?")
- **Structured itineraries:** Route timelines with times, places, descriptions

### What's missing:
- **No speakable schema** -- would help voice assistants cite content
- **No HowTo schema** on practical guides (bani etiquette, booking steps)
- **No Table schema** -- price comparison tables are HTML only, not schema-marked
- **article:modified_time present** but not on all articles
- **No ClaimReview or Fact schema** for verifiable claims (prices, distances)
- **No definitionbox/term markup** for local terms (kisi, khefi, etc.)

## 6. Duplicate Content Check

### Potential issues identified:
1. **RU/EN homepage:** Separate URLs with hreflang -- correct setup, no duplicate risk
2. **Bani article:** Has both `data-ru` and `data-en` attributes for client-side language switching on same URL -- this means Googlebot sees only RU content (correct), EN content loaded via JS fetch of en.json (not crawlable). Not a duplicate issue but EN content is invisible to search engines.
3. **Tour page kazbegi/** has similar but not identical content to blog **kazbegi-iz-tbilisi-2026/** -- this is acceptable (service page vs informational article) but internal linking should clearly differentiate intent
4. **FAQ content:** Some FAQ questions appear on both homepage and tour pages with similar answers -- minor overlap, acceptable for different landing pages

### No critical duplicate content issues detected.

## 7. AI-Generated Content Markers

Checked for low-quality AI content signals per Sept 2025 QRG:

- **Generic phrasing:** NOT detected. Content has specific prices, personal observations, named locations
- **First-hand experience:** STRONG. "I go once a month", "My tourists say...", "I usually stop here..."
- **Original insight:** PRESENT. Season recommendations based on 3 years of observation, specific restaurant recommendations with price ranges
- **Factual consistency:** Prices and distances are internally consistent across blog and tour pages
- **Repetitive structure:** Some structural repetition across blog articles (TOC + lead + sections + CTA + FAQ) -- this is template-driven but content varies per article. Acceptable.

**AI content risk: LOW.** Content reads as human-written with genuine experience markers. If AI-assisted in production, it has been properly enriched with first-hand details.

## 8. Key Recommendations (Priority Order)

### Critical (do first):

1. **Add author page at /about/** with full credentials, photo, verifiable claims (years guiding, languages, license number if applicable). Link all blog author-bylines to this page.

2. **Add sources block to bani-tbilisi article** (and all articles missing it). Reference official Georgian tourism sites, health data on sulfur springs.

3. **Fix bani-tbilisi broken meta tag** -- line 19 has malformed tag: `<meta https://sakhva-travel.com/images/blog/bani-tbilisi.webp">` -- missing `name="twitter:image" content=`.

### High priority:

4. **Expand bottom 3 tour pages** (soviet 1,190w, mtskheta 1,209w, slow-travel 1,298w) to 1,500+ words each with more route detail, seasonal info, and unique first-person observations.

5. **Add HowTo schema** to practical blog articles (bani etiquette, booking guides).

6. **Add external authority signals** to homepage: TripAdvisor badge/link, tourism association membership, press mentions if any.

7. **EN blog content invisible to crawlers** on bani-tbilisi (loaded via JS fetch). Either create separate /en/blog/bani-tbilisi/ URL or implement server-side rendering for EN content.

### Medium priority:

8. **Add article:modified_time** meta to all blog articles (only some have it).

9. **Create an author JSON-LD** entity with sameAs links (Google Maps profile, Instagram, WhatsApp business).

10. **Add speakable schema** to blog articles for voice search citation.

11. **Strengthen internal linking** from tour pages back to related blog articles (tour/kazbegi -> blog/kazbegi-iz-tbilisi-2026 link exists but reverse link is more prominent).

12. **Privacy policy and cancellation policy pages** should contain substantial content (not just legal boilerplate) -- these are trust signals Google evaluates for YMYL-adjacent pages (financial transactions).
